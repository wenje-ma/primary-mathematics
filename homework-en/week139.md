> **Definition 12.1.1 (Positive and Negative Numbers)**
> 
> 像 3, 1.8%, 3.5 这样大于 0 的数叫做正数; 像 -3, -2.7%, -4.5 这样在正数前加上负号的数叫做负数.

> **Definition 12.2.1 (折扣)**
> 
> 商品按原价的百分之几出售, 通常称打几折出售. 几折就表示十分之几, 也就是百分之几十.

> **Definition 12.2.2 (成数)**
> 
> 成数表示一个数是另一个数的十分之几, 通称"几成". 一成就是十分之一, 即 $10\%$; 二成就是 $20\%$; 三成五就是十分之三点五, 即 $35\%$.

> **Definition 12.2.3 (税率)**
> 
> 应缴纳的税款叫作应纳税额, 应纳税额与各种收入 (销售额, 营业额等) 中应纳税部分的比率叫作税率. 应纳税额 $=$ 收入中应纳税部分 $\times$ 税率.

> **Definition 12.2.4 (Interest Rate and Interest)**
> 
> 存入银行的钱叫做本金, 取款时银行多支付的钱叫做利息, 单位时间内利息与本金的比率叫做利率. 利息 = 本金 * 利率 * 存期.

> **Definition 12.3.1 (千分数与万分数)**
> 
> 千分数表示一个数是另一个数的千分之几, 也叫千分率, 千分号写作 "$\perthousand$". 万分数表示一个数是另一个数的万分之几, 也叫万分率, 万分号写作 "$\pertenthousand$". 它们与百分数一样, 只是把分母换成 $1000$ 或 $10000$.

> **Definition 12.4.1 (圆柱的认识)**
> 
> 圆柱由 $3$ 个面围成: 上, 下两个底面是大小完全相同的圆; 周围的面 (上, 下底面除外) 叫侧面, 是曲面; 两个底面之间的距离叫作高.

> **Definition 12.4.2 (圆柱的侧面积与表面积)**
> 
> 把圆柱侧面沿高展开得到一个长方形, 长方形的长等于圆柱底面的周长, 宽等于圆柱的高. 侧面积 $=$ 底面周长 $\times$ 高 $=2\pi{r}h$; 一个底面积 $=\pi{r}^{2}$; 表面积 $=$ 侧面积 $+$ 两个底面积.

> **Definition 12.4.3 (圆柱的体积)**
> 
> 把圆柱底面分成若干相等扇形, 切开后拼成一个近似长方体, 长方体底面积等于圆柱底面积, 高等于圆柱的高. 圆柱体积 $=$ 底面积 $\times$ 高 $=Sh=\pi{r}^{2}h$.

> **Definition 12.4.4 (圆锥的认识)**
> 
> 圆锥的底面是一个圆, 侧面是曲面; 从圆锥的顶点到底面圆心的距离是圆锥的高.

> **Definition 12.4.5 (圆锥的体积)**
> 
> 通过实验, 等底等高的圆锥形容器正好用 $3$ 次倒满圆柱形容器, 即圆锥体积是与它等底等高的圆柱体积的 $\frac{1}{3}$. 圆锥体积 $=\frac{1}{3}Sh=\frac{1}{3}\pi{r}^{2}h$.

## Exercise 1

(Lesson: Exercise 2, Problem 10) Mom has $1$ ten-thousand yuan and two investment options: one is a three-year government bond at an annual interest rate of $3.35\%$; the other is a one-year bank wealth-management product with an expected annual return of $3.6\%$, rolled over each year with principal and interest into the next year. If the expected return is realized, what is the difference in earnings between the two options after $3$ years?

### Solution to Exercise 1

By **Definition 12.2.4 (Interest Rate and Interest)**. Bond interest $=10000\times3.35\%\times3=1005$ (yuan). The wealth-management product rolls over principal plus interest each year: at the end of year 1, $10000\times1.036=10360$ yuan; at the end of year 2, $10360\times1.036=10732.96$ yuan; at the end of year 3, $10732.96\times1.036\approx11119.35$ yuan, giving earnings of about $11119.35-10000=1119.35$ yuan. The difference in earnings is $1119.35-1005=114.35$ (yuan). $\blacksquare$

## Exercise 2

(Lesson: Exercise 2, Problem 11) In 2021, China's output of LCD TVs was $17424.3$ ten-thousand sets, a growth of $-9.5\%$ over 2020. What was China's LCD TV output in 2020 in ten-thousand sets? (Round to one decimal place.)

### Solution to Exercise 2

By **Definition 12.1.1 (Positive and Negative Numbers)** and **Definition 12.2.2 (Tenths, or Cheng)**, a growth of $-9.5\%$ means 2021 was $9.5\%$ below 2020, i.e. $1-9.5\%=90.5\%$ of 2020. The 2020 output $=17424.3\div90.5\%\approx19253.4$ (ten thousand). $\blacksquare$

## Exercise 4

(Lesson: Activity 2) Aunt Li plans to deposit $5$ ten-thousand yuan, to be used six years later. The bank offers three investment options: ordinary savings deposits, buying government bonds, and buying wealth-management products. Government bonds have one-year, three-year, and five-year terms, among others; wealth-management products come in many kinds with varying rates. Investigate the current government-bond rates and the expected annual returns of wealth-management products, and help Aunt Li design a reasonable plan that maximizes her earnings after six years.

### Solution to Exercise 4

By **Definition 12.2.4 (Interest Rate and Interest)**, interest $=$ principal $\times$ interest rate $\times$ term. First investigate the current rates for bonds and wealth-management products, then compare different combinations: for example, roll a five-year bond into a one-year deposit after maturity, or reinvest the principal plus interest of a three-year bond for another three years. Within an acceptable risk, choose long-term high-rate products and arrange the maturity dates to maximize total interest over six years. Substitute each plan's principal, rate, and term into the interest formula to compute the total principal plus interest after six years, and take the maximum. $\blacksquare$

## Exercise 5

(Lesson: Did You Know?) In 2019, China's annual births were $1465$ ten-thousand people, a birth rate of $10.48\perthousand$; deaths were $998$ ten-thousand people, a death rate of $7.14\perthousand$; the natural growth rate was $3.34\perthousand$. Explain what each of these permille figures means.

### Solution to Exercise 5

By **Definition 12.3.1 (Permille and Permyriad)**, the birth rate $10.48\perthousand$ means that on average about $10.48$ people are born per $1000$ people; the death rate $7.14\perthousand$ means that on average about $7.14$ people die per $1000$ people; the natural growth rate $3.34\perthousand$ means that on average the population increases by about $3.34$ per $1000$ people (namely birth rate minus death rate: $10.48-7.14=3.34$). $\blacksquare$

## Exercise 8

(Lesson: Lateral Surface of a Cylinder, Do It) A cylindrical tea canister has a label around its lateral surface. The cylinder's base radius is $5\,\mathrm{cm}$ and its height is $20\,\mathrm{cm}$. When the label is unrolled it is a rectangle; what are its length and width in centimeters?

### Solution to Exercise 8

By **Definition 12.4.2 (Lateral Area and Surface Area of a Cylinder)**, the unrolled rectangle's length $=$ base circumference $=2\pi{r}=2\times3.14\times5=31.4\,\mathrm{cm}$; its width $=$ the cylinder's height $=20\,\mathrm{cm}$. As shown.

## Exercise 9

(Lesson: Exercise 3, Problem 3) Which of the following is the net of a cylinder? (Three combinations are given: circles paired with rectangles, with one side of the rectangle being $6.28,3,20\,\mathrm{cm}$, and so on.)

### Solution to Exercise 9

By **Definition 12.4.2 (Lateral Area and Surface Area of a Cylinder)**, one side of the rectangle in a cylinder's net must equal the circumference of the base circle. If the circle's diameter is $2\,\mathrm{cm}$, its circumference $=2\pi\approx6.28\,\mathrm{cm}$, so the rectangle whose length is $6.28\,\mathrm{cm}$ is the net of the cylinder. $\blacksquare$

## Exercise 10

(Lesson: Surface Area of a Cylinder, Do It) A cylindrical can has a label around its lateral surface. The cylinder's base radius is $5\,\mathrm{cm}$ and its height is $10\,\mathrm{cm}$. What is the area of the label?

### Solution to Exercise 10

By **Definition 12.4.2 (Lateral Area and Surface Area of a Cylinder)**, the label area $=$ lateral area $=2\pi{r}h=2\times3.14\times5\times10=314\,\mathrm{cm}^{2}$. $\blacksquare$

## Exercise 11

(Lesson: Surface Area of a Cylinder, Example 4) A chef's hat is approximately cylindrical, with height $30\,\mathrm{cm}$ and top diameter $20\,\mathrm{cm}$. About how many square centimeters of fabric are needed to make such a hat? (Round to the nearest ten.)

### Solution to Exercise 11

By **Definition 12.4.2 (Lateral Area and Surface Area of a Cylinder)**. The hat has only a lateral surface and a top (no bottom base). Lateral area $=\pi\times20\times30=1884\,\mathrm{cm}^{2}$; top area $=\pi\times\left(20\div2\right)^{2}=314\,\mathrm{cm}^{2}$; total $1884+314=2198\approx2200\,\mathrm{cm}^{2}$ (using rounding up). $\blacksquare$

## Exercise 12

(Lesson: Surface Area of a Cylinder, Do It, Problem 2) Find the lateral area of each cylinder below. (1) Base circumference $1.6\,\mathrm{m}$, height $0.7\,\mathrm{m}$. (2) Base radius $3.2\,\mathrm{dm}$, height $5\,\mathrm{dm}$.

### Solution to Exercise 12

## Exercise 13

(Lesson: Exercise 4, Problem 2) The front wheel of a road roller is cylindrical, with wheel width $2\,\mathrm{m}$ and diameter $1.2\,\mathrm{m}$. As the front wheel turns one full revolution, what area of road is rolled, in square meters?

### Solution to Exercise 13

By **Definition 12.4.2 (Lateral Area and Surface Area of a Cylinder)**, the area rolled in one revolution $=$ lateral area $=$ base circumference $\times$ wheel width $=3.14\times1.2\times2=7.536\,\mathrm{m}^{2}$. $\blacksquare$

## Exercise 14

(Lesson: Exercise 4, Problem 4) A certain drink can has base diameter $6\,\mathrm{cm}$ and height $12\,\mathrm{cm}$. Pack $24$ such cans in a box in $6$ columns and $4$ rows. What are the minimum length, width, and height of the box in centimeters?

### Solution to Exercise 14

Along the length place $6$ cans: length $=6\times6=36\,\mathrm{cm}$; along the width place $4$ cans: width $=4\times6=24\,\mathrm{cm}$; the height equals the can height $=12\,\mathrm{cm}$. $\blacksquare$
