> **Definition 8.8.1** (Mean) <br>The quotient obtained by dividing the sum of a set of data by the number of data points is called the mean of the set. Mean $=$ total quantity $\div$ number of parts. The mean reflects the overall situation of a data set well.
>
> **Definition 8.8.2** (Double Bar Chart) <br>A bar chart that uses two bars of different colors or shading to represent two sets of data is called a double bar chart. It clearly shows the amounts of the two data sets and makes comparison easy.

### Exercise 214

(Lesson: The Mean, Try It) $5$ students donated books: Yang Liang $8$, Wang Bo $6$, Liu Fei $9$, Zhang Li $8$, Tang Xiaodong $14$. On average, how many books did each student donate?

### Solution to Exercise 214

By **Definition 8.8.1** (Mean $=$ total $\div$ number of parts), $(8+6+9+8+14)\div5=45\div5=9$ (books). $\blacksquare$

### Exercise 227

(Lesson: Double Bar Chart, Practice 23, Problem 6) In a singing contest, contestant No. $6$ was scored by $7$ judges: $92,99,91,93,82,95,94$. Dropping the highest $99$ and the lowest $82$, what is the average score? Why drop the highest and lowest?

### Solution to Exercise 227

By **Definition 8.8.1** (Mean), $(92+91+93+95+94)\div5=465\div5=93$ (points). Dropping the extreme scores avoids one judge's too-high or too-low score skewing the average, making the result fairer. $\blacksquare$
