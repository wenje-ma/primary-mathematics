> **Definition 11.4.1** (Positive and Negative Numbers) <br>Numbers like $6$, $4.7$, $\frac{3}{5}$ are positive; numbers like $-6$, $-4.7$, $-\frac{3}{5}$ (with a leading "$-$" sign) are negative. $0$ is neither positive nor negative.
> 
> **Definition 11.4.2** (Positive/Negative Numbers for Opposite Quantities) <br>Positive and negative numbers express opposite quantities: declare one sense positive, and its opposite sense is negative. E.g. income positive, expense negative; temperature above zero positive, below zero negative; above sea level positive, below negative.

### Exercise 128

(Lesson: Finding Negative Numbers, Pre-class Survey) Find some numbers with "$-$" in daily life and learn their meanings, e.g. weather forecasts, electronic bills, altitude.

### Solution to Exercise 128

E.g. Beijing $-4\,^{\circ}\text{C}\sim0\,^{\circ}\text{C}$ means from $4$ below zero to $0$ degrees; the Turpan Basin's lowest point at $-154.31\,\text{m}$ altitude means $154.31\,\text{m}$ below sea level; a bill entry $-500.00$ yuan means a $500$ yuan expense. All use positive/negative numbers for opposite quantities. $\blacksquare$

### Exercise 129

(Lesson: Understanding Negative Numbers, Discuss) What is common to these "$-$" numbers (sub-zero temperature, below-sea-level altitude, expense)?

### Solution to Exercise 129

By **Definition 11.4.2** (Positive/Negative Numbers for Opposite Quantities), each uses a standard ($0\,^{\circ}\text{C}$, sea level, break-even) as a divider into two opposite senses, one marked positive, the other negative. $\blacksquare$

### Exercise 130

(Lesson: Understanding Negative Numbers, Think) Where on the number line should negative numbers go? Mark $-1$, $-2$, $-3$, $-4$. Is $0$ positive or negative?

The figure: a horizontal number line, with $0$ in the middle, $1,2,3,4$ marked to the right in order, and blank space to the left for marking $-1,-2,-3,-4$.

### Solution to Exercise 130

The line is like a sideways thermometer; negative numbers go left of $0$, and from $0$ leftward we mark $-1$, $-2$, $-3$, $-4$, with numbers decreasing to the left. By **Definition 11.4.1** (Positive and Negative Numbers), $0$ is neither. $\blacksquare$

### Exercise 131

(Lesson: Applying Negative Numbers, Do It) Spring Festival income/expense record: parents' wages $+9500$ yuan, to elders $-2000$ yuan, children's lucky money $-200$ yuan, New Year purchases $-500$ yuan. What do positives mean? Negatives? What is the month's result?

### Solution to Exercise 131

By **Definition 11.4.2** (Positive/Negative Numbers for Opposite Quantities), positives = income, negatives = expense. $9500-2000-200-500=6800$ yuan, i.e. a surplus of $6800$ yuan. $\blacksquare$

### Exercise 132

(Lesson: Applying Negative Numbers, Survey) How is the sit-and-reach score recorded? Test in class and record everyone's score.

### Solution to Exercise 132

Sit-and-reach takes palms just touching the toes as $0\,\text{cm}$; beyond the toes is positive, short of the toes is negative. After testing, record each person's score as a signed number. $\blacksquare$
