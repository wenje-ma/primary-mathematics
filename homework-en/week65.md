> **Definition 6.3.3 (周长)**
> 
> 封闭图形一周的长度, 是它的周长.

> **Definition 6.3.4 (长方形和正方形的周长)**
> 
> 参见教材相应章节关于此概念的论述.

> **Definition 6.4.1 (面积)**
> 
> 物体表面或封闭图形的大小, 是它们的面积. 比较面积大小要用统一的面积单位去测量, 看含有多少个这样的单位.

> **Definition 6.4.2 (面积单位)**
> 
> 常用面积单位有平方厘米 ($cm^2$), 平方分米 ($dm^2$), 平方米 ($m^2$). 边长 $1$ 厘米的正方形面积是 $1$ 平方厘米, 边长 $1$ 分米的正方形面积是 $1$ 平方分米, 边长 $1$ 米的正方形面积是 $1$ 平方米.

> **Definition 6.4.3 (长方形和正方形的面积)**
> 
> 长方形面积 $=$ 长$\times$宽; 正方形面积 $=$ 边长$\times$边长. 求面积看它包含多少个面积单位.

> **Definition 6.4.4 (面积单位间的进率)**
> 
> 相邻两个常用面积单位间的进率是 $100$: $1$ 平方分米 $=100$ 平方厘米, $1$ 平方米 $=100$ 平方分米.

> **Definition 6.5.1 (数据的收集与记录)**
> 
> 用举手, 填写问卷等方式收集数据, 用画"正"字记录: 一个"正"字有 $5$ 画, 代表 $5$ 个. 再把结果填入统计表, 每人只能选一项, 不重复不遗漏.

> **Definition 6.5.2 (复式统计表)**
> 
> 把两个 (或多个) 有联系的单式统计表合成一个表, 能同时表示多组数据, 便于比较和分析.

> **Definition 6.5.3 (数据的分段整理)**
> 
> 数据较多时, 按一定标准把数据分成几段整理, 这也是按标准分类. 用统计解决问题: 先收集整理数据, 再分析, 最后得出结论.

## Exercise 1

(Lesson: Practice 13, Problem 5) A sprinkler truck travels $200$ m per minute, watering a width of $8$ m; what area does it water in $5$ minutes?

### Solution to Exercise 1

Per minute it waters $200\times8=1600$ (square meters); in $5$ minutes, $1600\times5=8000$ (square meters).

$\blacksquare$

## Exercise 2

(Lesson: Practice 13, Problem 6) True or false: (1) $6$ square meters $=60$ square decimeters. (2) Using $8$ identical squares to form a rectangle has only one way. (3) Any figure formed by $8$ squares of $1$ square decimeter has area $8$ square decimeters.

### Solution to Exercise 2

(1) $\times$, $6$ square meters $=600$ square decimeters. (2) $\times$, $8=1\times8=2\times4$, so there are two ways. (3) $\surd$, however arranged, the total area is always $8$ squares of $1$ square decimeter.

$\blacksquare$

## Exercise 3

(Lesson: Practice 13, Problem 7) On grid paper each small cell is $1$ square centimeter; draw rectangles of perimeter $16$ cm. How many can you draw? Compute the areas and fill the table; what pattern do you find?

### Solution to Exercise 3

length$+$width$=16\div2=8$ (cm); you can draw: length $7$ width $1$ area $7$, length $6$ width $2$ area $12$, length $5$ width $3$ area $15$, length $4$ width $4$ (square) area $16$. Pattern: when the perimeter is equal, the closer the length and width, the larger the area.

$\blacksquare$

## Exercise 4

(Lesson: Practice 13, Problem 8) Around a square flower bed there is a $1$-m-wide cement path; the path's total area is $16$ square meters. What is the flower bed's area?

### Solution to Exercise 4

Let the flower bed's side be $a$ m; the outer square of the path has side $\left(a+2\right)$ m. $\left(a+2\right)^2-a^2=4a+4=16$, giving $a=3$ (m); the flower bed's area is $3\times3=9$ (square meters).

$\blacksquare$

## Exercise 9

(Lesson: Binning Data — Example) The one-minute jump-rope results of $30$ students in Class 1, Grade 3, sorted by boys and girls into excellent, good, pass and fail intervals; how many students are at each level?

### Solution to Exercise 9

By **Definition 6.5.3 (Binning Data into Intervals)**, first split by gender, then classify each result against the level ranges (girls: $\leqslant38$ fail, $39\sim108$ pass, $109\sim124$ good, $\geqslant125$ excellent; the boys' standard is slightly lower), count the numbers, and fill the two-way table.

$\blacksquare$
