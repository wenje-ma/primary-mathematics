> **Definition 12.6.1 (自行车里的数学)**<br>脚踏板带动前齿轮, 前齿轮带动后齿轮, 后齿轮带动后轮转动. 前齿轮齿数 $\times$ 前齿轮转数 $=$ 后齿轮齿数 $\times$ 后齿轮转数, 即后齿轮转数 $=\frac{\text{前齿轮齿数}}{\text{后齿轮齿数}}\times$ 前齿轮转数. 蹬一圈走的距离 $=\frac{\text{前齿轮齿数}}{\text{后齿轮齿数}}\times$ 车轮周长. 蹬同样圈数, 前后齿轮齿数的比值越大, 自行车走得越远.

## Exercise 91

(Lesson: Activity 1) For a bicycle, how far does it travel in one pedal revolution? Given the front chainring has $48$ teeth, the rear sprocket has $16$ teeth, and the wheel diameter is $60\,\mathrm{cm}$.

### Solution to Exercise 91

By **Definition 12.6.1 (Mathematics in a Bicycle)**, the rear sprocket (rear wheel) makes $=\frac{48}{16}=3$ revolutions. The wheel circumference $=\pi d=3.14\times60=188.4\,\mathrm{cm}$. One pedal revolution travels $3\times188.4=565.2\,\mathrm{cm}\approx5.65\,\mathrm{m}$. $\blacksquare$

## Exercise 92

(Lesson: Activity 2) A geared bicycle has front chainrings of $48$ and $40$ teeth and rear sprockets of $28, 24, 20, 18, 16, 14$ teeth. (1) How many different tooth ratios can be combined? (2) With the same number of pedal revolutions, which combination makes the bicycle travel farthest?

### Solution to Exercise 92

By **Definition 12.6.1 (Mathematics in a Bicycle)**. (1) There are $2$ front chainrings and $6$ rear sprockets, giving $2\times6=12$ combinations. (2) With the same number of pedal revolutions, the larger the front-to-rear tooth ratio, the farther it travels; the largest ratio is front $48$ paired with rear $14$, i.e. $48:14$, and this combination travels farthest. $\blacksquare$
