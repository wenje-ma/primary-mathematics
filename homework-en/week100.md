> **Definition 9.2.1 (小数乘整数)**
> 
> 先把小数转化为整数 (乘 $10,100,\cdots$), 按整数乘法算出积, 再把积除以相同的倍数, 点上小数点. 如 $9.5\times3$, 把 $9.5$ 看作 $95$ 角, $95\times3=285$ 角 $=28.5$ 元.

> **Definition 9.2.2 (小数乘小数)**
> 
> 先按整数乘法算出积, 再看两个乘数中一共有几位小数, 就从积的右边起数出几位, 点上小数点; 乘得的积的小数位数不够时, 要在前面用 $0$ 补足, 再点小数点.

> **Definition 9.2.3 (积的近似数)**
> 
> 根据需要, 按"四舍五入"法保留一定的小数位数, 求出积的近似数.

> **Definition 9.2.4 (倍数是小数)**
> 
> 一个数 ( $0$ 除外) 乘大于 $1$ 的数, 积比原来的数大; 乘小于 $1$ 的数, 积比原来的数小. 用小数表示倍的关系时, 也用乘法解决.

## Exercise 1

(Lesson: Extending the Integer Multiplication Laws to Decimals, Try It) Fill by the operation laws and calculate conveniently: $4.2\times1.69=\_\_\times\_\_$; $2.5\times\left(0.77\times0.4\right)=\left(\_\_\times\_\_\right)\times\_\_$; $7.2\times8.4+2.8\times8.4=\_\_\times\left(\_\_+\_\_\right)$; $101\times0.45$; $4.75\times99+4.75$; $0.034\times0.5\times0.6$; $2.73\times99$.

### Solution to Exercise 1

By **Definition 9.2.5 (Operation Laws Extended)**: $4.2\times1.69=1.69\times4.2$; $2.5\times\left(0.77\times0.4\right)=\left(2.5\times0.4\right)\times0.77$; $7.2\times8.4+2.8\times8.4=8.4\times\left(7.2+2.8\right)=84$. Convenient calculation: $101\times0.45=\left(100+1\right)\times0.45=45+0.45=45.45$; $4.75\times99+4.75=4.75\times\left(99+1\right)=475$; $0.034\times0.5\times0.6=0.034\times0.3=0.0102$; $2.73\times99=2.73\times\left(100-1\right)=273-2.73=270.27$.

$\blacksquare$

## Exercise 3

(Lesson: Decimal Multiplication, Practice 3, Review and Consolidation, Problem 2) Calculate conveniently: $0.78\times98$; $4.8\times0.25$; $0.5\times2.33\times8$; $56\times1.25$; $1.2\times2.5+0.8\times2.5$; $0.8\times0.25\times0.4\times12.5$.

### Solution to Exercise 3

By **Definition 9.2.5 (Operation Laws Extended)**: $0.78\times98=0.78\times\left(100-2\right)=78-1.56=76.44$; $4.8\times0.25=1.2\times4\times0.25=1.2$; $0.5\times2.33\times8=0.5\times8\times2.33=9.32$; $56\times1.25=7\times8\times1.25=70$; $1.2\times2.5+0.8\times2.5=\left(1.2+0.8\right)\times2.5=5$; $0.8\times0.25\times0.4\times12.5=\left(0.8\times12.5\right)\times\left(0.25\times0.4\right)=10\times0.1=1$.

$\blacksquare$

## Exercise 8

(Lesson: Solving Problems, Estimation, Example) Mom has $100$ yuan for shopping: $2$ bags of rice at $30.98$ yuan each, and $0.8$ kg of meat at $26.48$ yuan per kg. Is the remaining money enough for a $10$-yuan box of eggs? For a $20$-yuan box?

### Solution to Exercise 8

By **Definition 9.2.6 (Estimation)**, estimating upward: $31\times2+27\times0.8=62+21.6=83.6<89$, so the actual total is less than $89$ yuan; estimating downward: $30\times2+25\times0.8=60+20=80$, so the actual total is more than $80$ yuan. The remaining money is between $11$ and $20$ yuan, enough for the $10$-yuan eggs but not the $20$-yuan eggs.

$\blacksquare$

## Exercise 9

(Lesson: Solving Problems, Tiered Billing, Example) A taxi charges $8$ yuan for $3$ km and within, and $1.5$ yuan per km beyond $3$ km (less than $1$ km billed as $1$ km). How much for a $6.3$ km ride?

### Solution to Exercise 9

By **Definition 9.2.7 (Tiered Billing)**, $6.3$ km is billed as $7$ km, and the excess is $7-3=4$ km. Cost $=8+1.5\times4=8+6=14$ yuan.

$\blacksquare$

## Exercise 11

(Lesson: Solving Problems, Try It, Problem 2) Tap water is billed in tiers: within $12$ t, $2.5$ yuan per ton; beyond $12$ t, $3.8$ yuan per ton. (1) Xiao Yun's family used $11$ t; how much? (2) Xiao Ke's family used $17$ t; how much?

### Solution to Exercise 11

By **Definition 9.2.7 (分段计费)**: (1) $11<12$, $11\times2.5=27.5$ yuan. (2) $12\times2.5+\left(17-12\right)\times3.8=30+19=49$ yuan.

$\blacksquare$

## Exercise 13

(Lesson: Decimal Multiplication, Practice 4, Integrated Use, Problem 1) A room is $8.1$ m long and $5.2$ m wide, to be tiled with $0.6$ m square tiles; are $100$ tiles enough? (Ignore waste.)

### Solution to Exercise 13

Room area $8.1\times5.2=42.12$ m$^2$. Each tile $0.6\times0.6=0.36$ m$^2$; $100$ tiles total $36$ m$^2<42.12$ m$^2$, so not enough.

$\blacksquare$

## Exercise 15

(Lesson: Decimal Multiplication, Practice 4, Extension and Improvement, Problem 2) The result of $1.\square2\times5.3$ might be ( ): A. $7.526$; B. $0.996$; C. $11.176$; D. $7.562$.

### Solution to Exercise 15

By **Definition 9.2.2 (小数乘小数)**, $1.\square2\times5.3$ has three decimal places, ruling out B ($0.996<1\times5.3=5.3$, too small). Estimate the range: $1.02\times5.3=5.406$, $1.92\times5.3=10.176$. A $7.526$ ends in $6=2\times3$, correct; D $7.562$ ends in $2\neq2\times3=6$, wrong. Choose A or C; substituting to check: $1.42\times5.3=7.526$, choose A.

$\blacksquare$
