> **Definition 11.6.1** (Lane Circumference Difference) <br>On a standard $400\,\text{m}$ track the straightaways are identical in every lane; the difference lies in the semicircular turns. Adjacent lanes are $1.25\,\text{m}$ wide, so the outer turn radius is $1.25\,\text{m}$ larger, and adjacent lanes differ by $1.25\times2\pi\approx7.85\,\text{m}$ per lap.

### Exercise 199

(Lesson: The 400 m Track, Pre-class Survey) Gather data on your school or a nearby stadium's track; describe its shape and parts.

### Solution to Exercise 199

A track has two straights and two semicircular turns; the straights are equal and the turns together form one circle; there are several lanes, each wider than the one inside. $\blacksquare$

### Exercise 200

(Lesson: The 400 m Track, Do It Problem 1) The innermost turn radius is about $36.3\,\text{m}$, each straight about $85.96\,\text{m}$; together, about how long is the innermost lap? What is the second lane's turn radius? (Lane width $1.25\,\text{m}$.)

### Solution to Exercise 200

The turns make one circle: $2\times3.14\times36.3\approx228.1\,\text{m}$; total $85.96\times2+228.1\approx400\,\text{m}$. Second lane radius $36.3+1.25=37.55\,\text{m}$. $\blacksquare$

### Exercise 201

(Lesson: The 400 m Track, Do It Problem 2) What is the circumference difference between adjacent lanes? Compute the lap lengths for lanes $1\sim6$.

### Solution to Exercise 201

By **Definition 11.6.1** (Lane Circumference Difference), adjacent lanes differ by $1.25\times2\times3.14=7.85\,\text{m}$. The $n$-th lap length $=400+(n-1)\times7.85\,\text{m}$. $\blacksquare$

### Exercise 202

(Lesson: Why Starting Lines Differ, Think) In a $400\,\text{m}$ race, by how many meters should each lane's starting line be ahead of the previous? In a $200\,\text{m}$ race?

### Solution to Exercise 202

A $400\,\text{m}$ lap covers two semicircular turns (one full circle); by **Definition 11.6.1** (Lane Circumference Difference) advance by $7.85\,\text{m}$; a $200\,\text{m}$ race covers one semicircular turn, so advance $7.85\div2=3.925\,\text{m}$. $\blacksquare$
