> **Definition 8.2.1** (Observing Objects from Different Directions) <br>When observing an object built from small cubes from the front, top, left side (or right side), the planar figure seen is called the shape seen from that direction. Looking at the same object from different directions generally gives different figures; looking at different objects from the same position may also give the same figure.
