> **Definition 11.1.1 (有序数对)**
> 
> 用两个数表示平面内一个点的位置, 第一个数表示"列", 第二个数表示"行", 记作 $\left(a,b\right)$. 同一列的点有序数对第一个数相同, 同一行的点第二个数相同.
> **Definition 11.1.2 (方向和距离确定位置)**
> 
> 确定平面内一个物体的准确位置, 先确定参照点, 再知道物体相对于参照点的方向和距离. 方向一般用不大于 $45^{\circ}$ 的角描述, 如"北偏西 $40^{\circ}$"; 平面图上线段长度按比例尺表示实际距离.

## Exercise 7

(Lesson: Ordered Pairs, Exercise 1 Integrated Problem 2) A sorting robot delivers parcels: picks up at $\left(3,0\right)$, goes to $\left(3,1\right)$, then horizontally to $\left(7,1\right)$, then vertically to $\left(7,8\right)$, finally delivers at $\left(8,8\right)$. (1) From chute ③ to the Jiangxi delivery point, what other route? (2) To the Zhejiang delivery point? (3) With multiple robots delivering at once, how to avoid collisions?

### Solution to Exercise 7

The robot can only move horizontally or vertically. By **Definition 11.1.1 (Ordered Pairs)**, moving along a row changes the first number; moving along a column changes the second. (1) You may also go horizontal first then vertical, e.g. $\left(3,0\right)\to\left(8,0\right)\to\left(8,8\right)$, as long as the route avoids other chutes; (2) similarly go in horizontal/vertical segments to the Zhejiang point; (3) assign different robots different times or lanes so no two share a point at any moment. $\blacksquare$

## Exercise 9

(Lesson: Direction and Distance, Example 2) A fishing boat is $1500\,\text{m}$ from the rescue ship. How to describe its position? The boat is on the east-by-south $25^{\circ}$ bearing (or south-by-east $65^{\circ}$). On the map $1\,\text{cm}$ represents $500\,\text{m}$, so $1500\,\text{m}$ is drawn as $3\,\text{cm}$.

The figure: a bearing diagram with the rescue ship as observation point, showing a direction marker (north up, south down, west left, east right); from the rescue ship a segment $3\,\text{cm}$ long is drawn in the east-by-south $25^{\circ}$ direction, the endpoint is marked as the fishing boat, and the angle $25^{\circ}$ and scale are labeled.

### Solution to Exercise 9

Distance alone doesn't fix position. By **Definition 11.1.2 (Determining Position by Direction and Distance)**: with the rescue ship as reference, the boat lies east-by-south $25^{\circ}$ at distance $1500\,\text{m}$. The two descriptions' angles sum to $90^{\circ}$; by convention use the angle no more than $45^{\circ}$, i.e. east-by-south $25^{\circ}$. $\blacksquare$

## Exercise 13

### Solution to Exercise 13
