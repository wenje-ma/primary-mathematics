> **Definition 8.3.1 (加法交换律)**
> 
> 两个数相加, 交换两个加数的位置, 和不变. 用字母表示: $a+b=b+a$.

> **Definition 8.3.2 (加法结合律)**
> 
> 三个数相加, 先把前两个数相加, 或者先把后两个数相加, 和不变. 用字母表示: $\left(a+b\right)+c=a+\left(b+c\right)$.

> **Definition 8.3.3 (减法的运算性质)**
> 
> 一个数连续减去两个数, 等于减去这两个数的和: $a-b-c=a-\left(b+c\right)$; 也可以先减后一个减数再减前一个: $a-b-c=a-c-b$.

> **Definition 8.3.4 (乘法交换律)**
> 
> 两个数相乘, 交换两个因数的位置, 积不变. 用字母表示: $a\times{b}=b\times{a}$.

> **Definition 8.3.5 (乘法结合律)**
> 
> 三个数相乘, 先乘前两个数, 或者先乘后两个数, 积不变. 用字母表示: $\left(a\times{b}\right)\times{c}=a\times\left(b\times{c}\right)$.

> **Definition 8.3.6 (乘法分配律)**
> 
> 两个数的和与一个数相乘, 可以先把它们与这个数分别相乘, 再相加. 用字母表示: $\left(a+b\right)\times{c}=a\times{c}+b\times{c}$, 也可反过来 $a\times{c}+b\times{c}=\left(a+b\right)\times{c}$.

> **Definition 8.3.7 (除法的运算性质)**
> 
> 一个数连续除以两个数, 等于除以这两个除数的积: $a\div{b}\div{c}=a\div\left(b\times{c}\right)$.

> **Definition 8.3.8 (简便计算)**
> 
> 利用加法, 乘法的运算律和减法, 除法的运算性质, 把能凑成整十, 整百的数先算, 使计算更简便.

## Exercise 1

(Lesson: Convenient Addition and Subtraction, Practice 6, Problem 2) On the $3$rd month (March) the $6$th Aunt Wang received a transfer of $225.00$ yuan, on the $3$rd month the $10$th she received $328.00$ yuan, and on the $3$rd month the $11$th she received $175.00$ yuan. How much transfer money did she receive in total over these days?

### Solution to Exercise 1

By **Definition 8.3.8 (Convenient Calculation)**, $225+328+175=\left(225+175\right)+328=400+328=728$ (yuan). $\blacksquare$

## Exercise 2

(Lesson: Convenient Addition and Subtraction, Practice 6, Problem 3) A mountain is $2000\,\mathrm{m}$ above sea level, passing in turn two points at elevations $416\,\mathrm{m}$ and $284\,\mathrm{m}$. Find the elevation of the other point.

### Solution to Exercise 2

By **Definition 8.3.3 (Operation Property of Subtraction)**, $2000-416-284=2000-\left(416+284\right)=2000-700=1300$ ($\mathrm{m}$). $\blacksquare$

### Solution to Exercise 3

## Exercise 4

(Lesson: Convenient Addition and Subtraction, Practice 6, Problem 5) Calculate using a suitable method: (1) $1+2+3+4+\cdots+98+99+100$; (2) $2+4+6+\cdots+18+20$; (3) $5+10+15+\cdots+95+100$; (4) $20-19+18-17+\cdots+4-3+2-1$.

### Solution to Exercise 4

(1) Pair the first and last: $\left(1+100\right)\times100\div2=5050$. (2) $\left(2+20\right)\times10\div2=110$. (3) $\left(5+100\right)\times20\div2=1050$. (4) Group in pairs: $\left(20-19\right)+\left(18-17\right)+\cdots+\left(2-1\right)=1\times10=10$. $\blacksquare$

## Exercise 5

(Lesson: Convenient Addition and Subtraction, Practice 6, Problem 6) Complete the table of numbers of students in the upper grades of a primary school: Grade 3 $\left(36,37,34\right)$, Grade 4 $\left(34,31,39\right)$, Grade 5 $\left(35,36,35\right)$, Grade 6 $\left(38,32,\_\_\right)$ with a total of $108$. Find the total for each grade and the number in Grade 6 Class 3.

### Solution to Exercise 5

Grade 3: $36+37+34=107$ (people); Grade 4: $34+31+39=104$ (people); Grade 5: $35+36+35=106$ (people); Grade 6 Class 3: $108-38-32=38$ (people). $\blacksquare$

## Exercise 6

(Lesson: Convenient Addition and Subtraction, Practice 6, Problem 7) Teacher Wang has $68$ essays to grade. On the first day she graded $22$, and on the second day she graded $8$ more than on the first day. How many essays are left ungraded?

### Solution to Exercise 6

On the second day she graded $22+8=30$ (essays), graded in total $22+30=52$ (essays), leaving $68-52=16$ (essays). $\blacksquare$

## Exercise 7

(Lesson: Convenient Addition and Subtraction, Practice 6, Problem 8) In the second quarter a mall sold $375$ air conditioners of brands A, B and C altogether; brand A sold $125$ units and brand B sold $75$ units. How many units of brand C were sold?

### Solution to Exercise 7

By **Definition 8.3.3 (Operation Property of Subtraction)**, $375-125-75=375-\left(125+75\right)=375-200=175$ (units). $\blacksquare$

## Exercise 8

(Lesson: Convenient Addition and Subtraction, Practice 6, Problem 9) A sample television is now $2255$ yuan, reduced by $355$ yuan for the holiday, and the sample price is reduced by another $245$ yuan. What was the original price of this television?

### Solution to Exercise 8

By **Definition 8.3.2 (Associative Law of Addition)**, original price $=2255+355+245=2255+600=2855$ (yuan). $\blacksquare$

## Exercise 9

(Lesson: Operation Laws of Multiplication, Try It) Fill in the blanks by the operation laws of multiplication: $12\times32=32\times$ ___; $30\times6\times7=30\times(6\times$ ___ $)$; $125\times\left(8\times4\right)=($ ___ $\times$ ___ $)\times$ ___; $108\times75=$ ___ $\times$ ___.

### Solution to Exercise 9

By **Definition 8.3.4 (Commutative Law of Multiplication)** and **Definition 8.3.5 (Associative Law of Multiplication)**: $12\times32=32\times12$; $30\times6\times7=30\times\left(6\times7\right)$; $125\times\left(8\times4\right)=\left(125\times8\right)\times4$; $108\times75=75\times108$. $\blacksquare$

## Exercise 10

(Lesson: Distributive Law of Multiplication, Try It) Which of the following expressions are correct? Mark correct ones with "√" and wrong ones with "×": $56\times\left(19+28\right)=56\times19+28$; $32\times\left(7\times3\right)=32\times7+32\times3$; $64\times64+36\times64=\left(64+36\right)\times64$.

### Solution to Exercise 10

By **Definition 8.3.6 (Distributive Law of Multiplication)**: $56\times\left(19+28\right)=56\times19+28$ is wrong, it should be $56\times19+56\times28$; $32\times\left(7\times3\right)=32\times7+32\times3$ is wrong, this is a continued product and should use the associative law $32\times7\times3$; $64\times64+36\times64=\left(64+36\right)\times64$ is correct. $\blacksquare$

### Solution to Exercise 12
