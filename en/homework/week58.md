> **Definition 8.1.1 (加法的意义和各部分间的关系)**<br>把两个数合并成一个数的运算, 叫作加法. 相加的两个数叫作加数, 加得的数叫作和. 和 $=$ 加数 $+$ 加数, 加数 $=$ 和 $-$ 另一个加数.
> **Definition 8.1.2 (减法的意义和各部分间的关系)**<br>已知两个加数的和与其中一个加数, 求另一个加数的运算, 叫作减法. 在减法中, 已知的和叫作被减数. 减法是加法的逆运算. 差 $=$ 被减数 $-$ 减数, 被减数 $=$ 减数 $+$ 差, 减数 $=$ 被减数 $-$ 差.
> **Definition 8.1.3 (乘法的意义和各部分间的关系)**<br>求几个相同加数的和的简便运算, 叫作乘法. 相乘的两个数叫作因数, 乘得的数叫作积. 积 $=$ 因数 $\times$ 因数, 因数 $=$ 积 $\div$ 另一个因数.
> **Definition 8.1.4 (除法的意义和各部分间的关系)**<br>已知两个因数的积与其中一个因数, 求另一个因数的运算, 叫作除法. 在除法中, 已知的积叫作被除数. 除法是乘法的逆运算. 商 $=$ 被除数 $\div$ 除数, 除数 $=$ 被除数 $\div$ 商, 被除数 $=$ 商 $\times$ 除数.
> **Definition 8.1.5 (有关 0 的运算)**<br>一个数加上 $0$ 还得原数; 被减数等于减数时, 差是 $0$; 一个数和 $0$ 相乘仍得 $0$; $0$ 除以一个非 $0$ 的数还得 $0$. $0$ 不能作除数.
> **Definition 8.1.6 (有余数除法各部分间的关系)**<br>在有余数的除法里, 被除数 $=$ 商 $\times$ 除数 $+$ 余数, 余数要比除数小.
> **Definition 8.1.7 (四则混合运算的顺序)**<br>加, 减, 乘, 除四种运算统称四则运算. 一个算式里, 既有小括号又有中括号, 要先算小括号里面的, 再算中括号里面的, 最后算括号外面的.
> **Definition 8.1.8 (租船租车问题的策略)**<br>解决怎样租船 (租车) 最省钱的问题, 先算每种船 (车) 每个座位的单价, 优先租单价便宜的, 再调整尽量不空座位, 比较几种方案后选最便宜的.

## Exercise 1

(Lesson: Meaning of Addition and Subtraction and Their Parts, Try It) Based on $2468+575=3043$, write the answers to the following two directly: $3043-2468=$ ___; $3043-575=$ ___.

### Solution to Exercise 1

## Exercise 2

(Lesson: Meaning of Addition and Subtraction and Their Parts, Practice 1, Problem 1) Which method should be used to calculate the following, and why? (1) A ski resort sold $86$ tickets in the morning and $59$ tickets in the afternoon. How many tickets were sold in total for the whole day? (2) A total of $145$ tickets were sold for the whole day, of which $86$ were sold in the morning. How many were sold in the afternoon? (3) A stationery store sold $370$ exercise books and had $630$ left. How many were purchased in total? (4) A primary school has $843$ students, of whom $418$ are boys. How many girls are there?

### Solution to Exercise 2

(1) By **Definition 8.1.1 (Meaning of Addition and the Relationships Among Its Parts)**, combine the morning and afternoon with addition: $86+59=145$ (tickets). (2) By **Definition 8.1.2 (Meaning of Subtraction and the Relationships Among Its Parts)**, given the total and one part, find the other part with subtraction: $145-86=59$ (tickets). (3) Sold plus remaining equals purchased, use addition: $370+630=1000$ (books). (4) Total minus boys gives girls, use subtraction: $843-418=425$ (people). $\blacksquare$

## Exercise 21

(Lesson: Solving Problems, Practice 3, Problem 4) How to rent cars most cheaply? Each large car seats $40$ people at a rental of $900$ yuan; each small car seats $20$ people at a rental of $500$ yuan. For this spring outing there are $18$ teachers and $322$ students.

### Solution to Exercise 21

Total people $18+322=340$ (people). Per seat: large car $900\div40=22.5$ (yuan), small car $500\div20=25$ (yuan), so the large car is cheaper. By **Definition 8.1.8 (Strategy for Boat/Car-Rental Problems)**, $340\div40=8$ (cars) $\cdots\cdots20$ (people), and the remaining $20$ people exactly fill $1$ small car with no empty seats: $8\times900+1\times500=7700$ (yuan). Renting $8$ large cars and $1$ small car is the cheapest. $\blacksquare$
