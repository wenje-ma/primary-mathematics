> **Definition 6.2.1 (口算除法)**
> 
> 整十, 整百数除以一位数, 把被除数看成几个十, 几个百再除; 两位数除以一位数, 分成整十数和个位数分别除再相加. 如 $60\div3=20$, $69\div3=23$, $600\div3=200$.
> **Definition 6.2.2 (除法估算)**
> 
> 把被除数看成与它接近的整十, 整百数再除, 只估出大约结果, 不用算出准确值. 如 $283\div3$ 在 $90$ 与 $100$ 之间.
> **Definition 6.2.3 (笔算除法法则)**
> 
> 从被除数的高位除起: 先用除数试除被除数最高位, 比除数小再试前两位; 除到哪一位, 商就写在那一位上面; 不够商 $1$ 就商 $0$ 占位; 每求出一位商, 余下的数必须比除数小.
> **Definition 6.2.4 (除法的验算)**
> 
> 没有余数时, 商$\times$除数$=$被除数; 有余数时, 商$\times$除数$+$余数$=$被除数.
> **Definition 6.2.5 (0 的除法)**
> 
> $0$ 除以任何不是 $0$ 的数都得 $0$; $0$ 不能作除数.
> **Definition 6.2.6 (用乘除法解决两步问题)**
> 
> 分析数量关系, 可从条件出发想能求什么, 也可从问题出发想先求什么, 分步列式或列综合算式. 归一问题先求单一量, 归总问题先求总量.

## Exercise 1

(Lesson: Solving Problems — Do It, Problem 2) One kind of cup comes $6$ to a box and $8$ boxes to a carton. How many cartons can $960$ cups be packed into?

### Solution to Exercise 1

By **Definition 6.2.6 (Totalization Problem)**, $960\div6\div8=20$ (cartons), or $960\div\left(6\times8\right)=20$ cartons. $\blacksquare$

## Exercise 2

(Lesson: Practice 5, Problem 1) A fruit store newly received $3$ boxes of apples, $25$ kg per box, at a purchase price of $3$ yuan per kg. How much did these apples cost in total?

### Solution to Exercise 2

By **Definition 6.2.6 (Totalization Problem)**, $3\times25\times3=225$ (yuan). $\blacksquare$

## Exercise 3

(Lesson: Practice 5, Problem 2) Xiaoming's family has $4$ people, each producing $12$ kg of waste per month on average. How many kg in half a year ($6$ months)?

### Solution to Exercise 3

By **Definition 6.2.6 (Totalization Problem)**, $4\times12\times6=288$ (kilograms). $\blacksquare$

## Exercise 4

(Lesson: Practice 5, Problem 3) A tofu shop bought $8$ bags of soybeans, $50$ kg per bag; $1$ kg of soybeans makes about $4$ kg of tofu. About how many kg of tofu can these soybeans make?

### Solution to Exercise 4

By **Definition 6.2.6 (Totalization Problem)**, $8\times50\times4=1600$ (kilograms). $\blacksquare$

## Exercise 5

(Lesson: Practice 5, Problem 4) A bottle of medicine has $150$ tablets, taken $3$ times a day, $2$ tablets each time. How many days will this bottle last?

### Solution to Exercise 5

By **Definition 6.2.6 (Totalization Problem)**, per day $3\times2=6$ (tablets), $150\div6=25$ (days). $\blacksquare$

## Exercise 6

(Lesson: Practice 5, Problem 5) There are $756$ books and $3$ bookshelves, each with $4$ shelves. How many books on each shelf on average?

### Solution to Exercise 6

By **Definition 6.2.6 (Totalization Problem)**, $756\div3\div4=63$ (books). $\blacksquare$

## Exercise 7

(Lesson: Practice 5, Problem 6) The pool is $25$ meters long; Xiaojun swam $4$ round trips. How many meters did he swim in total?

### Solution to Exercise 7

One round trip is $2$ one-way lengths; $4$ round trips are $4\times2=8$ one-way lengths; $25\times8=200$ (meters). $\blacksquare$

## Exercise 9

(Lesson: Practice 5, Problem 8) $96$ dolls, $3$ per box, $8$ boxes per carton. (1) How many cartons are needed to pack the dolls? (2) Raise a two-step multiplication question and answer it.

### Solution to Exercise 9

(1) $96\div3\div8=4$ (cartons). (2) For example, "$9$ yuan per box, how much are these dolls worth in total", $96\div3\times9=288$ yuan; answers may vary. $\blacksquare$

## Exercise 10

(Lesson: Practice 5, Problem 9) Xiaoming collects specimens for $4$ hours a day; in $2$ days he collects $72$ specimens. (1) How many specimens per hour on average? (2) To collect $224$ specimens, can he finish in $7$ days?

### Solution to Exercise 10

(1) $72\div2\div4=9$ (specimens). (2) In $7$ days he can collect $9\times4\times7=252$ (specimens), $252> 224$, so he can finish. $\blacksquare$

## Exercise 11

(Lesson: Unit-Rate and Total-Amount — Do It, Problem 1) $5$ trucks carry $25$ tons per trip. (1) At this rate, how many tons do $9$ trucks carry per trip? (2) How many trucks are needed to carry $120$ tons in one trip?

### Solution to Exercise 11

By **Definition 6.2.6 (unit rate)**, each truck carries $25\div5=5$ (tons). (1) $5\times9=45$ (tons); (2) $120\div5=24$ (trucks). $\blacksquare$

## Exercise 12

(Lesson: Unit-Rate and Total-Amount — Do It, Problem 2) A batch of computers gives exactly $3$ per class to $15$ classes. If each class gets $5$, how many classes can be served?

### Solution to Exercise 12

By **Definition 6.2.6 (total amount)**, there are $3\times15=45$ (computers) in total, $45\div5=9$ (classes). $\blacksquare$

## Exercise 13

(Lesson: Practice 6, Problem 1) $4$ boxes of bees make $48$ kg of honey. At this rate, how many kg do $9$ boxes make?

### Solution to Exercise 13

By **Definition 6.2.6 (unit rate)**, each box makes $48\div4=12$ (kg), $12\times9=108$ (kg). $\blacksquare$

## Exercise 14

(Lesson: Practice 6, Problem 2) A snail crawls $8$ meters in $2$ hours. At this rate, how many meters in $5$ hours?

### Solution to Exercise 14

Per hour it crawls $8\div2=4$ (meters), $4\times5=20$ (meters). $\blacksquare$

## Exercise 15

(Lesson: Practice 6, Problem 3) A book of $15$ pages a day is finished in $4$ days. (1) If reading $6$ pages a day, in how many days is it finished? (2) Finished in $3$ days, how many pages per day on average?

### Solution to Exercise 15

By **Definition 6.2.6 (total amount)**, there are $15\times4=60$ (pages) in total. (1) $60\div6=10$ (days); (2) $60\div3=20$ (pages). $\blacksquare$

## Exercise 16

(Lesson: Practice 6, Problem 4) $20$ kg of peanuts yield $5$ kg of oil. At this rate, how many kg of oil do $300$ kg of peanuts yield?

### Solution to Exercise 16

$300\div20\times5=75$ (kilograms), or $5\div20\times300=75$ kilograms. $\blacksquare$

## Exercise 17

(Lesson: Practice 6, Problem 5) Xiaoli used sticks to make $8$ of one figure. Using the same sticks to make another figure, how many can be made in total? (sides not shared)

### Solution to Exercise 17

First find the total number of sticks (sticks per figure $\times8$), then divide by the sticks needed for the other figure; the quotient is the number that can be made. $\blacksquare$

## Exercise 18

(Lesson: Practice 6, Problem 6) Fill the table: (1) pencil cases at the same unit price, quantities $5,11,12$ correspond to totals $45,54,72$; (2) a car travels the same distance; speed $84$ corresponds to time $4$, find the speed $?$ corresponding to times $5,6,7$ and the speed corresponding to time $6$.

### Solution to Exercise 18

## Exercise 21

(Lesson: Review and Consolidation — Say It) Using eggs as an example, state the quantitative relations: (1) a box has $2$ rows of $4$ eggs each; how many in $2$ boxes? (2) $16$ eggs in $2$ boxes, each box $2$ rows; how many per row? (3) $16$ eggs exactly fill $2$ boxes; how many boxes for $96$ eggs? (4) a small box holds $6$, a large box holds $8$; $40$ small boxes are used, how many large boxes?

### Solution to Exercise 21

By **Definition 6.2.6 (Totalization Problem)**: (1) $4\times2\times2=16$ (eggs); (2) $16\div2\div2=4$ (eggs); (3) $16\div2=8$ (eggs per box), $96\div8=12$ (boxes); (4) total $6\times40=240$ (eggs), $240\div8=30$ (boxes). $\blacksquare$
