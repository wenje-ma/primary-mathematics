> **Definition 8.4.1 (小数的意义和计数单位)**
> 
> 把整体 1 平均分成 10 份,100 份,1000 份, 这样的一份或几份可以用分母是 10,100,1000 的分数来表示, 也可以用小数表示.

> **Definition 8.6.1 (小数加减法)**
> 
> 计算小数减法时, 先把小数点对齐, 再按照整数减法的法则进行计算.

> **Definition 8.6.2 (小数加减混合运算的顺序)**
> 
> 在没有括号的算式里, 有乘除法和加减法, 要先算乘除法; 算式里有括号, 要先算括号里面的.

> **Definition 8.6.3 (整数加法运算律推广到小数)**
> 
> 整数加法的交换律, 结合律对小数加法同样适用, 应用运算律可以使一些小数计算更简便.

> **Definition 8.7.1 (轴对称图形)**
> 
> 轴对称图形中, 对称点到对称轴的距离相等.

> **Definition 8.7.2 (平移)**
> 
> 平移不改变图形的形和大小, 只改变图形的位置.

> **Definition 8.7.3 (用平移求面积和周长)**
> 
> 运用平移的方法可以将不规则图形转化为规则图形来求面积.

> **Definition 8.8.1 (平均数)**
> 
> 一组数据的和除以这组数据的个数所得的商, 叫作这组数据的平均数. 平均数 $=$ 总数量 $\div$ 总份数. 平均数能较好地反映一组数据的总体情况.

> **Definition 8.8.2 (复式条形统计图)**
> 
> 复式条形统计图可以同时表示多组数据, 便于直观比较.

## Exercise 1

(Lesson: Extending the Integer Addition Laws to Decimals, Practice 19, Problem 6) A kind of sock: buy $5$ pairs get $1$ pair free, each pair $4.68$ yuan. Aunt Zhang wants $12$ pairs; how much will she spend?

### Solution to Exercise 1

Buy $5$ get $1$ free; buying $10$ pairs gets $2$ free, exactly $12$ pairs, so only pay for $10$ pairs: $4.68\times10=46.8$ (yuan). $\blacksquare$

## Exercise 2

(Lesson: Extending the Integer Addition Laws to Decimals, Practice 19, Problem 7) An object is dropped from a height and hits the ground after $4$ seconds. In the $1$st second it falls $4.9\,\mathrm{m}$, and each later second it falls $9.8\,\mathrm{m}$ farther than the previous second. How high above the ground was the object before being dropped?

### Solution to Exercise 2

The $2$nd second: $4.9+9.8=14.7\,\mathrm{m}$; the $3$rd second: $24.5\,\mathrm{m}$; the $4$th second: $34.3\,\mathrm{m}$. Total $4.9+14.7+24.5+34.3=78.4$ ($\mathrm{m}$). $\blacksquare$

## Exercise 20

(Lesson: The Mean, Try It) $5$ students donated books: Yang Liang $8$, Wang Bo $6$, Liu Fei $9$, Zhang Li $8$, Tang Xiaodong $14$. On average, how many books did each student donate?

### Solution to Exercise 20

By **Definition 8.8.1 (Mean $=$ total $\div$ number of parts)**, $\left(8+6+9+8+14\right)\div5=45\div5=9$ (books). $\blacksquare$

## Exercise 21

(Lesson: The Mean, Practice 22, Problem 1) The week's temperatures: Monday $10\sim21^\circ\mathrm{C}$, Tuesday $10\sim21^\circ\mathrm{C}$, Wednesday $12\sim22^\circ\mathrm{C}$, Thursday $12\sim24^\circ\mathrm{C}$, Friday $11\sim22^\circ\mathrm{C}$, Saturday $11\sim21^\circ\mathrm{C}$, Sunday $11\sim23^\circ\mathrm{C}$. Fill in the average high and average low.

### Solution to Exercise 21

By **Definition 8.8.1 (Mean)**: average high $\left(21+21+22+24+22+21+23\right)\div7=154\div7=22^\circ\mathrm{C}$; average low $\left(10+10+12+12+11+11+11\right)\div7=77\div7=11^\circ\mathrm{C}$. $\blacksquare$
