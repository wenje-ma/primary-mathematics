> **Definition 8.3.8 (简便计算)**
> 
> 利用加法, 乘法的运算律和减法, 除法的运算性质, 把能凑成整十, 整百的数先算, 使计算更简便.

> **Definition 8.4.1 (小数的意义和计数单位)**
> 
> 把整体 1 平均分成 10 份,100 份,1000 份, 这样的一份或几份可以用分母是 10,100,1000 的分数来表示, 也可以用小数表示.

> **Definition 8.4.2 (小数的数位顺序)**
> 
> 小数点左边是整数部分 (个位, 十位, 百位 $\cdots$), 右边是小数部分 (十分位, 百分位, 千分位 $\cdots$), 十分位的计数单位是十分之一, 百分位是百分之一.

> **Definition 8.4.3 (小数的读法和写法)**
> 
> 读小数时, 整数部分按整数读法读, 小数点读作"点", 小数部分依次读出每个数字; 写小数时, 小数部分依次写出每个数字.

> **Definition 8.4.4 (小数的性质)**
> 
> 小数的末尾添上"$0$"或去掉"$0$", 小数的大小不变. 应用这一性质可以化简小数或按需要改写小数.

> **Definition 8.4.5 (小数的大小比较)**
> 
> 先比较整数部分, 整数部分大的那个数就大; 整数部分相同的, 十分位上的数大的就大; 十分位也相同的, 百分位上的数大的就大 $\cdots$

> **Definition 8.4.6 (小数点移动引起小数大小的变化)**
> 
> 小数点向右移动一位, 两位, 三位 $\cdots$ 原数分别乘 $10$, $100$, $1000\cdots$ 扩大到原数的 $10$ 倍, $100$ 倍, $1000$ 倍; 向左移动一位, 两位 $\cdots$ 原数分别除以 $10$, $100\cdots$ 缩小到原数的 $\frac{1}{10}$, $\frac{1}{100}$.

> **Definition 8.4.7 (小数与单位换算)**
> 
> 把低级单位的数改写成高级单位的数要除以进率, 把高级单位的数改写成低级单位的数要乘进率, 可以利用小数点移动的规律.

> **Definition 8.4.8 (小数的近似数)**
> 
> 用"四舍五入"法保留一定的小数位数. 保留整数精确到个位, 保留一位小数精确到十分位, 保留两位精确到百分位. 表示近似数时, 小数末尾的 $0$ 不能去掉.

> **Definition 8.4.9 (把较大数改写成用万或亿作单位的数)**

## Exercise 1

(Lesson: Meaning, Reading and Writing of Decimals, Practice 9, Problem 7) Mark the positions of the following numbers on a number line: $0.4$, $1.6$, $2.3$, $3.85$.

### Solution to Exercise 1

By **Definition 8.4.1 (Meaning of Decimals)**, each $1$ large interval on the line is $1$, divided equally into $10$ small intervals of $0.1$ each. $0.4$ is the $4$th small interval after $0$, $1.6$ is the $6$th small interval after $1$, $2.3$ is the $3$rd small interval after $2$, and $3.85$ is exactly midway between $3.8$ and $3.9$. $\blacksquare$

## Exercise 2

(Lesson: Meaning, Reading and Writing of Decimals, Practice 9, Problem 8) Shade to represent the following decimals: $0.6$, $1.7$, $0.05\,\mathrm{m}$.

### Solution to Exercise 2

By **Definition 8.4.1 (Meaning of Decimals)**, for $0.6$ divide a square equally into $10$ parts and shade $6$; for $1.7$ fill $1$ whole square and shade $7$ parts of another; $0.05\,\mathrm{m}=5\,\mathrm{cm}$, so on a $1\,\mathrm{m}$ segment shade a $5\,\mathrm{cm}$ length. $\blacksquare$

## Exercise 3

(Lesson: Meaning, Reading and Writing of Decimals, Practice 9, Problem 9) Explain the meaning of the following decimals: $8.6$ yuan; $3.5\,\mathrm{m}$; $0.4\,\mathrm{kg}$; $400\,\mathrm{g}$.

### Solution to Exercise 3

$8.6$ yuan means $8$ yuan $6$ jiao; $3.5\,\mathrm{m}$ means $3\,\mathrm{m}\,5\,\mathrm{dm}$; $0.4\,\mathrm{kg}$ means dividing $1\,\mathrm{kg}$ equally into $10$ parts and taking $4$, i.e. $400\,\mathrm{g}$. $\blacksquare$

## Exercise 4

(Lesson: Meaning, Reading and Writing of Decimals, Practice 9, Problem 10) Using $3$, $0$, $8$, $5$ and the decimal point, write the following numbers, using each digit once: (1) a decimal less than $1$ with three decimal places; (2) a decimal greater than $8$ with three decimal places; (3) a decimal in which $0$ is not read aloud and which has two decimal places.

### Solution to Exercise 4

(1) Integer part is $0$: $0.358$, $0.385$, $0.538$, etc.; (2) integer part is $8$: $8.035$, $8.053$, $8.305$, etc.; (3) $0$ is in the ones place and not read aloud, e.g. $30.58$, $50.38$, $80.35$, etc. $\blacksquare$

## Exercise 5

(Lesson: Property of Decimals, Try It) Simplify the following: $0.70$; $105.0900$.

### Solution to Exercise 5

By **Definition 8.4.4 (Property of Decimals)**, remove the trailing $0$: $0.70=0.7$; $105.0900=105.09$. $\blacksquare$

## Exercise 6

(Lesson: Property of Decimals, Try It) Without changing the size, write the following as three-decimal-place numbers: $0.2$; $4.08$; $3$.

### Solution to Exercise 6

By **Definition 8.4.4 (Property of Decimals)**, append $0$ at the end: $0.2=0.200$; $4.08=4.080$; $3=3.000$. $\blacksquare$

## Exercise 7

(Lesson: Comparing Decimals, Try It) Compare the two numbers in each group: $3$ yuan $\bigcirc 2.6$ yuan; $6.35\,\mathrm{m}\bigcirc 6.53\,\mathrm{m}$; $4.79\bigcirc 4.723$; $0.458\bigcirc 0.54$.

### Solution to Exercise 7

By **Definition 8.4.5 (Comparing Decimals)**: $3$ yuan $> 2.6$ yuan; $6.35\,\mathrm{m}< 6.53\,\mathrm{m}$; $4.79> 4.723$; $0.458< 0.54$. $\blacksquare$

## Exercise 8

(Lesson: Property and Comparison of Decimals, Practice 10, Problem 1) Without changing the size, which "$0$" in the numbers below can be removed and which cannot? $3.90\,\mathrm{m}$; $0.30$ yuan; $500\,\mathrm{m}$; $1.80$ yuan; $0.70\,\mathrm{m}$; $0.04$ yuan; $600\,\mathrm{kg}$; $20.20\,\mathrm{m}$.

### Solution to Exercise 8

By **Definition 8.4.4 (Property of Decimals)**, only trailing $0$'s of a decimal can be removed: the trailing $0$ in $3.90\,\mathrm{m}$, $0.30$ yuan, $1.80$ yuan, $0.70\,\mathrm{m}$, $20.20\,\mathrm{m}$ can be removed; $500\,\mathrm{m}$ and $600\,\mathrm{kg}$ are trailing $0$'s of integers, and the $0$ in $0.04$ yuan is not at the end, so none of these can be removed. $\blacksquare$

## Exercise 9

(Lesson: Property and Comparison of Decimals, Practice 10, Problem 2) Connect the equal numbers: $2.70$, $4.400$, $31.0100$, $0.005$, $72.060$, $2.07$, $0.0050$, $31.01$, $4.40$, $72.60$.

### Solution to Exercise 9

## Exercise 10

(Lesson: Property and Comparison of Decimals, Practice 10, Problem 3) If "$0$" is appended to the end of the following numbers, which keep the same size and which change? $3.4$, $18$, $0.06$, $700$, $3.0$, $908$, $150$, $104.03$, $10.01$, $42.00$.

### Solution to Exercise 10

By **Definition 8.4.4 (Property of Decimals)**, appending $0$ to the end of a decimal keeps its size: $3.4$, $0.06$, $3.0$, $104.03$, $10.01$, $42.00$ keep the same size; the integers $18$, $700$, $908$, $150$ become larger when $0$ is appended. $\blacksquare$

## Exercise 11

(Lesson: Property and Comparison of Decimals, Practice 10, Problem 4) Without changing the size, rewrite the following as decimals with three decimal places: $3.6$; $0.27$; $10.8$; $5.0500$; $40$; $0.4050$.

### Solution to Exercise 11

## Exercise 13

(Lesson: Property and Comparison of Decimals, Practice 10, Problem 6) Fill in the $\bigcirc$ with "$> $", "$< $" or "$=$": $7.9\bigcirc8.2$; $0.51\bigcirc0.509$; $1.374\bigcirc1.3$; $5.7\bigcirc5.8$; $0.6\bigcirc0.60$; $1.23\bigcirc1.32$.

## Exercise 14

(Lesson: Property and Comparison of Decimals, Practice 10, Problem 7) The prices of three products in stores A, B and C: product 1: $28.98$, $28.89$, $29.00$ yuan; product 2: $3.40$, $3.35$, $3.30$ yuan; product 3: $3.50$, $3.55$, $3.45$ yuan. Which store is cheapest for each product?

### Solution to Exercise 14

## Exercise 15

## Exercise 16

(Lesson: Property and Comparison of Decimals, Practice 10, Problem 9) Sort by weight from heaviest to lightest: Xiao Jun $38.5\,\mathrm{kg}$, Xiao Li $37.8\,\mathrm{kg}$, Xiao Fang $43.6\,\mathrm{kg}$, Xiao Gang $43.9\,\mathrm{kg}$.

### Solution to Exercise 16
