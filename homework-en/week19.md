> **Definition 3.2.1 (乘法的含义)**
> 
> 求几个相同加数的和, 可以用乘法表示. 如 $5$ 个 $3$ 相加, 写成 $3\times5=15$, 读作"$3$ 乘 $5$ 等于 $15$".

> **Definition 3.2.2 (乘法算式各部分名称)**
> 
> 乘号两边的数都叫乘数, 乘得的结果叫积. 如在 $3\times5=15$ 中, $3$ 和 $5$ 是乘数, $15$ 是积.

> **Definition 3.2.3 (乘法口诀)**
> 
> 乘法口诀是计算表内乘法的基础, 每句口诀对应两个乘法算式 (如二三得六对应 2*3=6 和 3*2=6).

> **Definition 3.2.4 (乘加,乘减)**
> 
> 形如 $3\times3+2$,$3\times4-1$ 的算式叫乘加,乘减; 在这样的算式中, 要先算乘法, 再算加法或减法.

> **Definition 3.2.5 (乘法解决问题)**
> 
> 求几个几是多少, 用乘法计算; 把相同加数连加的问题, 都可以用乘法简便地解决.

### Solution to Exercise 1

## Exercise 2

(Lesson: An Introduction to Multiplication, Example, Circles) How many circles are there in total? Write an expression and solve. Looking vertically, each column has $4$ and there are $6$ columns; looking horizontally, each row has $6$ and there are $4$ rows. What do you notice?

### Solution to Exercise 2

By **Definition 3.2.1 (The Meaning of Multiplication)**, vertically it is $6$ copies of $4$ added: $4+4+4+4+4+4=24$, i.e. $4\times6=24$; horizontally it is $4$ copies of $6$ added: $6+6+6+6=24$, i.e. $6\times4=24$. Notice that the factors of the two expressions have swapped places, while the product is the same. $\blacksquare$

## Exercise 5

(Lesson: Practice, Problem 1) Match them as in the example: connect "$2$ copies of $3$ added", $3+3+5$, "factors $6$ and $4$", "$5$ times $3$", $6+4$, $3+2$, $5+5+5$, $9+9+9+9$, and so on, with the corresponding multiplication expressions.

### Solution to Exercise 5

By **Definition 3.2.1 (The Meaning of Multiplication)** and **Definition 3.2.2 (Names of the Parts of a Multiplication Expression)**, only additions with identical addends can be written as multiplication: "$2$ copies of $3$ added" connects to $3\times2$; "factors $6$ and $4$" connects to $6\times4$; "$5$ times $3$" connects to $5\times3$; $5+5+5$ connects to $5\times3$; $9+9+9+9$ connects to $9\times4$. $3+3+5$, $6+4$, and $3+2$ do not have identical addends, so they connect to no multiplication. $\blacksquare$

## Exercise 7

(Lesson: Practice, Problem 3) Who is right? Explain why. "The sum of $2$ copies of $6$ can be written as $6\times6$." "The result of $3$ copies of $4$ added is the same as the result of $4$ copies of $3$ added."

### Solution to Exercise 7

By **Definition 3.2.1 (The Meaning of Multiplication)**, the first statement is wrong: $2$ copies of $6$ added should be written as $6\times2$, not $6\times6$; the second statement is right: $3$ copies of $4$ is $4\times3=12$, $4$ copies of $3$ is $3\times4=12$, and the results are the same. $\blacksquare$

## Exercise 10

(Lesson: Practice, Problem 7) Can you compute these using addition? $9\times4$, $8\times5$, $15\times3$, $2\times18$.

### Solution to Exercise 10

By **Definition 3.2.1 (The Meaning of Multiplication)**, rewrite each multiplication as repeated addition of identical addends: $9\times4=9+9+9+9=36$; $8\times5=8+8+8+8+8=40$; $15\times3=15+15+15=45$; $2\times18=18+18=36$. $\blacksquare$

## Exercise 11

(Lesson: Practice, Problem 8) Fill in. $5\times6+5=5\times\_\_$; $2\times4-2=2\times\_\_$.

### Solution to Exercise 11

By **Definition 3.2.4 (Multiplication-Addition and Multiplication-Subtraction)**, $5\times6+5$ means $6$ copies of $5$ plus another $1$ copy of $5$, which is $7$ copies of $5$, i.e. $5\times7$; $2\times4-2$ means $4$ copies of $2$ with $1$ copy of $2$ removed, which is $3$ copies of $2$, i.e. $2\times3$. $\blacksquare$
