> **Definition 7.4.1 (口算乘法)**
> 
> 两位数乘一位数的口算, 先把两位数分成整十数和一位数, 分别相乘后再相加.

> **Definition 7.4.2 (笔算乘法)**
> 
> 多位数乘多位数时, 把相同数位对齐, 从个位乘起, 用第二个因数的每一位分别乘第一个因数, 再把所得的积相加.

> **Definition 7.4.3 (积的变化规律)**
> 
> 一个因数不变, 另一个因数乘 (或除以) 几 (0 除外), 积也乘 (或除以) 相同的数.

> **Definition 7.4.4 (乘法估算)**
> 
> 根据问题需要把乘数估大或估小, 快速判断结果范围, 再决定精确计算.

## Exercise 1

(Lesson: How the Product Changes — Think About It) A rectangular green area is $200$ m$^2$, width $8$ m; the length is unchanged and the width increases to $24$ m; what is the enlarged area?

### Solution to Exercise 1

By **Definition 7.4.3 (How the Product Changes)**, the width goes from $8$ m to $24$ m, multiplied by $3$, and the length is unchanged, so the area is also multiplied by $3$: $200\times3=600$ m$^2$.

$\blacksquare$

## Exercise 9

(Lesson: Practice 9, Problem 7) Compute the first column and find the pattern to write the second: $15\times15$, $25\times25$, $35\times35$, $45\times45$; $55\times55$, $65\times65$, $75\times75$, $85\times85$.

### Solution to Exercise 9

Pattern: when two equal numbers whose ones digit is $5$ are multiplied, the last two digits are $25$, and the front is the tens digit times (the tens digit $+1$). $15\times15=225$, $25\times25=625$, $35\times35=1225$, $45\times45=2025$, $55\times55=3025$, $65\times65=4225$, $75\times75=5625$, $85\times85=7225$.

## Exercise 11

(Lesson: Estimation in Multiplication — Do It) $1026$ teachers and students take buses, each limited to $48$ people (including the driver); are $19$ buses enough?

### Solution to Exercise 11

Each bus actually carries $47$ teachers/students, $47\times19\approx50\times20=1000<1026$, not enough. Computing exactly, $47\times19=893<1026$, not enough.

$\blacksquare$

## Exercise 12

(Lesson: Practice 10, Problem 1) To type a $3000$-character article, Uncle Ding types $105$ characters per minute; can he finish in $30$ minutes?

### Solution to Exercise 12

$105\times30=3150$ characters, $3150>3000$, he can finish.

$\blacksquare$

## Exercise 13

(Lesson: Practice 10, Problem 2) The library has $4$ study rooms, each with $19$ rows and $18$ people per row; about how many can it hold at once?

### Solution to Exercise 13

By **Definition 7.4.4 (Estimation in Multiplication)**, $19\times18\approx20\times20=400$, $400\times4=1600$ people.

$\blacksquare$

## Exercise 14

(Lesson: Practice 10, Problem 3) $293$ boxes of honey peaches at $18$ kg per box; can a truck with a load capacity of $6$ t carry them in one trip?

### Solution to Exercise 14

$293\times18\approx300\times20=6000$ kg $=6$ t; computing exactly, $293\times18=5274$ kg $<6000$ kg, it can carry them.
