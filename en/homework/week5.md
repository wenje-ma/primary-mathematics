# Assignment 5

> **Definition 1.3.1** (Cuboid and Cube) <br>A cuboid has flat faces and sits steadily on a desk; a cube has square faces of the same size, and also has flat faces.
> 
> **Definition 1.3.2** (Cylinder and Sphere) <br>A cylinder has circular top and bottom faces; it can stand upright or roll; a sphere can roll in all directions.

### Exercise 125

(Lesson: Recognizing Solid Shapes, Sort It) Sort by shape: what shape are these objects?

### Solution to Exercise 125

By **Definition 1.3.1** (Cuboid and Cube) and **Definition 1.3.2** (Cylinder and Sphere), classify the objects into four categories: cuboids (e.g. soap), cubes, cylinders (e.g. cans), and spheres. $\blacksquare$
