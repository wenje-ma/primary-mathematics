> **Definition 6.2.1 (口算除法)**
> 
> 整十, 整百数除以一位数, 把被除数看成几个十, 几个百再除; 两位数除以一位数, 分成整十数和个位数分别除再相加. 如 $60\div3=20$, $69\div3=23$, $600\div3=200$.

> **Definition 6.2.2 (除法估算)**
> 
> 把被除数看成与它接近的整十, 整百数再除, 只估出大约结果, 不用算出准确值. 如 $283\div3$ 在 $90$ 与 $100$ 之间.

> **Definition 6.2.3 (笔算除法法则)**
> 
> 从被除数的高位除起: 先用除数试除被除数最高位, 比除数小再试前两位; 除到哪一位, 商就写在那一位上面; 不够商 $1$ 就商 $0$ 占位; 每求出一位商, 余下的数必须比除数小.

> **Definition 6.2.4 (除法的验算)**
> 
> 没有余数时, 商$\times$除数$=$被除数; 有余数时, 商$\times$除数$+$余数$=$被除数.

> **Definition 6.2.5 (0 的除法)**
> 
> $0$ 除以任何不是 $0$ 的数都得 $0$; $0$ 不能作除数.

> **Definition 6.2.6 (用乘除法解决两步问题)**
> 
> 分析数量关系, 可从条件出发想能求什么, 也可从问题出发想先求什么, 分步列式或列综合算式. 归一问题先求单一量, 归总问题先求总量.

## Exercise 1

(Lesson: Practice 3, Problem 14) Toys are $5$ yuan and $6$ yuan; stationery is $8$ yuan and $9$ yuan. Teacher Li spent $114$ yuan on one kind of toy and $125$ yuan on one kind of stationery. (1) Which of each did she buy, and how many? (2) If this money is spent only on pencil cases, how many can be bought and how much is left?

### Solution to Exercise 1

(1) By **Definition 6.2.6 (Totalization Problem)**, divide the money spent by each unit price; the one that divides exactly is what was bought. $114\div6=19$, so the $6$-yuan toy was bought $19$ times; then divide $125$ by each stationery unit price, and the one dividing exactly gives the stationery bought and its count. (2) The total is $114+125=239$ yuan; $239\div$ pencil-case price, the quotient is the count and the remainder the money left. $\blacksquare$

## Exercise 2

(Lesson: Practice 3, Problem 15) Xiaohong opened a book; the sum of the page numbers on the two facing pages is $185$. Which two pages are open?

### Solution to Exercise 2

The two facing pages are consecutive numbers, the right page being $1$ more than the left. $\left(185-1\right)\div2=92$, $92+1=93$; pages $92$ and $93$ are open. $\blacksquare$

## Exercise 11

(Lesson: Practice 4, Problem 4) A pair of rattan chairs costs $404$ yuan, and $4$ dining chairs cost $424$ yuan. How much more is each rattan chair than each dining chair?

### Solution to Exercise 11

Each rattan chair is $404\div2=202$ (yuan), each dining chair is $424\div4=106$ (yuan), $202-106=96$ (yuan), so it is $96$ yuan more expensive. $\blacksquare$

## Exercise 12

### Solution to Exercise 12

## Exercise 13

(Lesson: Practice 4, Problem 6) Xiaoming writes $609$ large characters in $3$ weeks, and Xiaoliang writes $964$ in $4$ weeks. How many more per week does Xiaoliang write than Xiaoming on average?

### Solution to Exercise 13

Xiaoming per week: $609\div3=203$ (characters); Xiaoliang per week: $964\div4=241$ (characters); $241-203=38$ (characters), so $38$ more. $\blacksquare$

## Exercise 16

(Lesson: Practice 4, Problem 9) True or false: (1) A three-digit number divided by a one-digit number does not always give a three-digit quotient. (2) However many $0$s are at the end of the dividend, the quotient must have that many $0$s at the end. (3) Number A $\div$ Number B $=25\cdots\cdots6$, and Number A is at most $181$.

### Solution to Exercise 16

(1) $\surd$; for example $312\div4$ gives a two-digit quotient. (2) $\times$; for example $400\div8=50$, the dividend has two $0$s but the quotient has only one $0$. (3) $\times$; the remainder is $6$, so Number B must be greater than $6$, at most the one-digit number $9$, giving Number A $=9\times25+6=231$, not $181$. $\blacksquare$

## Exercise 17

(Lesson: Practice 4, Problem 10) In a division expression, the quotient and the remainder are both $3$, and the divisor is exactly $3$ times the remainder. What is the dividend?

### Solution to Exercise 17

The divisor is $=3\times3=9$. By **Definition 6.2.4 (Checking Division)**, the dividend is $=9\times3+3=30$. $\blacksquare$
