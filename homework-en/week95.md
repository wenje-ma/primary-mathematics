> **Definition 8.6.1 (小数加减法)**
> 
> 计算小数减法时, 先把小数点对齐, 再按照整数减法的法则进行计算.

> **Definition 8.8.1 (平均数)**
> 
> 一组数据的和除以这组数据的个数所得的商, 叫作这组数据的平均数. 平均数 $=$ 总数量 $\div$ 总份数. 平均数能较好地反映一组数据的总体情况.

> **Definition 8.8.2 (复式条形统计图)**
> 
> 复式条形统计图可以同时表示多组数据, 便于直观比较.

> **Definition 8.9.1 (营养午餐的标准)**
> 
> $10$ 岁左右的儿童从每顿午餐中获取的热量应不低于 $2926$ 千焦, 脂肪应不超过 $50\,\mathrm{g}$. 搭配午餐时要同时满足热量和脂肪两项要求.

## Exercise 2

(Lesson: The Mean, Practice 22, Problem 4) $6$ students' heights: $139,140,135,138,139,137\,\mathrm{cm}$; weights: $34,38,35,34,36,33\,\mathrm{kg}$. Find the average height and average weight.

### Solution to Exercise 2

By **Definition 8.8.1 (Mean)**: average height $\left(139+140+135+138+139+137\right)\div6=828\div6=138\,\mathrm{cm}$; average weight $\left(34+38+35+34+36+33\right)\div6=210\div6=35\,\mathrm{kg}$.

$\blacksquare$

## Exercise 3

(Lesson: The Mean, Practice 22, Problem 5) Two groups do sit-ups: the first group of $4$ people did $132$ in total, the second group of $5$ people did $155$ in total. Which group performed better?

### Solution to Exercise 3

The numbers of people differ, so by **Definition 8.8.1 (Mean)** compare the averages: first group $132\div4=33$ (sit-ups), second group $155\div5=31$ (sit-ups). The first group performed better.

$\blacksquare$

## Exercise 4

(Lesson: The Mean, Practice 22, Problem 6) A fruit store's strawberry sales over $7$ days: $12,11,11,10,12,9,9\,\mathrm{kg}$. (1) How many kilograms are sold per day on average? (2) As a buyer, how much should be stocked on day $25$?

### Solution to Exercise 4

(1) By **Definition 8.8.1 (Mean)**, $\left(12+11+11+10+12+9+9\right)\div7=74\div7\approx10.6\,\mathrm{kg}$. (2) Stock about $10\sim11\,\mathrm{kg}$ per day on average, adjusting according to the sales trend.

## Exercise 11

(Lesson: Double Bar Chart, Practice 23, Problem 6) In a singing contest, contestant No. $6$ was scored by $7$ judges: $92,99,91,93,82,95,94$. Dropping the highest $99$ and the lowest $82$, what is the average score? Why drop the highest and lowest?

### Solution to Exercise 11

By **Definition 8.8.1 (Mean)**, $\left(92+91+93+95+94\right)\div5=465\div5=93$ (points). Dropping the extreme scores avoids one judge's too-high or too-low score skewing the average, making the result fairer.

$\blacksquare$

## Exercise 13

(Lesson: Nutritious Lunch) Each lunch combines $3$ different dishes. How many qualifying lunches can you make? (Energy no less than $2926$ kilojoules, fat no more than $50\,\mathrm{g}$.)

### Solution to Exercise 13

By **Definition 8.9.1 (Standards for a Nutritious Lunch)**, take any $3$ of the $9$ dishes and keep those whose energy sums to at least $2926$ and fat to at most $50$. For example, fried chicken cutlet $1254+$ tomato scrambled eggs $899+$ mushroom bok choy $911=3064$ kilojoules, fat $19+15+11=45\,\mathrm{g}\le50\,\mathrm{g}$, which qualifies. Count all qualifying combinations by this standard.

$\blacksquare$
