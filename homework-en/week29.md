> **Definition 3.7.1 (7 的乘法口诀)**
> 
> 一七得七, 二七十四, 三七二十一, 四七二十八, 五七三十五, 六七四十二, 七七四十九. 相邻两句口诀的积相差 $7$; 一句口诀一般可计算两个乘法算式.

> **Definition 3.7.2 (8 的乘法口诀)**
> 
> 一八得八, 二八十六, 三八二十四, 四八三十二, 五八四十, 六八四十八, 七八五十六, 八八六十四. 相邻两句口诀的积相差 $8$.

> **Definition 3.7.3 (9 的乘法口诀)**
> 
> 一九得九, 二九十八, 三九二十七, 四九三十六, 五九四十五, 六九五十四, 七九六十三, 八九七十二, 九九八十一. 几个九就是几个十减几, 如 $9\times5$ 就是 $50-5=45$; 积的十位数字与个位数字之和都是 $9$.

> **Definition 3.7.4 (用 7~9 的乘法口诀求商)**
> 
> 计算除数是 $7,8,9$ 的除法时, 想"除数和几相乘得被除数", 那句乘法口诀中缺失的乘数就是商. 如 $56\div8$, 想"七八五十六", 商是 $7$.

> **Definition 3.7.5 (乘除两步解决问题)**
> 
> 先用乘法求出总数, 再用除法求每份数或份数; 前一步算出的结果就是后一步的条件, 两步缺一不可.

> **Definition 3.7.6 (乘法口诀表)**
> 
> 小九九口诀表共 $45$ 句, 呈三角形排列, 从"一一得一"到"九九八十一"; 除两个乘数相同的口诀外, 每句口诀可计算两个乘法算式和两个除法算式.

## Exercise 5

(Lesson: Solving Problems, Example, Amusement Park) A group of students first ride the Ferris wheel, $4$ people per cabin, filling exactly $3$ cabins; then they ride bumper cars, $2$ people per car. (1) How many people in total? (2) How many bumper cars are needed?

### Solution to Exercise 5

By **Definition 3.7.5 (Two-Step Multiplication-Division Problems)**: (1) $4\times3=12$ (people); (2) $12\div2=6$ (cars). The result of the first question, $12$ people, is the given condition for the second. $\blacksquare$

## Exercise 7

(Lesson: Solving Problems, Do It, Problem 2) Jumping on a number line: a frog jumps $3$ squares each time, a rabbit jumps $6$ squares, a kangaroo jumps $18$ squares. One rabbit jump equals how many frog jumps? One kangaroo jump equals how many frog jumps? $2$ kangaroo jumps equal how many rabbit jumps? How many copies of $3$ are in $6$? How many copies of $3$ are in $9$? How many copies of $3$ and how many copies of $6$ are in $18$?

### Solution to Exercise 7

$6\div3=2$, one rabbit jump equals $2$ frog jumps; $18\div3=6$, one kangaroo jump equals $6$ frog jumps; $18\times2=36$, $36\div6=6$, $2$ kangaroo jumps equal $6$ rabbit jumps. $6$ contains $2$ copies of $3$; $9$ contains $3$ copies of $3$; $18$ contains $6$ copies of $3$ and $3$ copies of $6$. $\blacksquare$

## Exercise 10

(Lesson: Solving Problems, Practice, Problem 2) The school arranges potted flowers into a certain number of identical patterns. Arranging $9$ pots per pattern needs $63$ pots, but there are not enough pots, so only $8$ pots can make one pattern. How many patterns can be arranged? How many pots are used in the end?

### Solution to Exercise 10

Number of patterns: $63\div9=7$ (patterns). Changed to $8$ pots per pattern, $7$ patterns need $8\times7=56$ pots. So $7$ patterns are arranged, and $56$ pots are used in the end. $\blacksquare$

## Exercise 13

(Lesson: Solving Problems, Think, Problem 1) Think and fill in: based on the grid picture (8 copies of $2$, 4 copies of $4$, 2 copies of $8$, 1 copy of $16$), write four $\square\times\square=16$; then fill in $16\div\square=2$, $16\div\square=4$, $16\div\square=8$, $16\div\square=16$.
