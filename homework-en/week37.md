> **Definition 4.4.1 (计数单位的十进关系)**
> 
> 一个一个地数, $10$ 个一是十; 十个十个地数, $10$ 个十是一百; $10$ 个一百是一千; $10$ 个一千是一万. 个 (一), 十, 百, 千, 万都是计数单位, 相邻两个间都是 $10$ 倍.

> **Definition 4.4.2 (数位与数位顺序)**
> 
> 从右边起依次是个位, 十位, 百位, 千位, 万位 $\cdots\cdots$ 哪一位上是几, 就表示有几个这样的计数单位. 如百位上的 $5$ 表示 $5$ 个百.

> **Definition 4.4.3 (万以内数的读法)**
> 
> 从高位读起, 按数位顺序读: 千位是几读几千, 百位是几读几百 $\cdots\cdots$ 中间有一个 $0$ 或两个 $0$, 只读一个零; 末尾不管有几个 $0$, 都不读.

> **Definition 4.4.4 (万以内数的写法)**
> 
> 从高位写起, 按数位顺序写: 几千就在千位写几, 几百就在百位写几 $\cdots\cdots$ 中间或末尾哪一位上一个也没有, 就在那一位上写 $0$.

> **Definition 4.4.5 (比较万以内数的大小)**
> 
> 位数不同, 位数多的数大; 位数相同, 从最高位比起, 最高位上数大的那个就大, 最高位相同就比下一位, 直到比出大小.

> **Definition 4.4.6 (近似数与约等号)**
> 
> 生活中有时不用准确数而用近似数. 接近整千, 整百的数可写成近似数, 用约等号 $\approx$ 连接, 读作约等于. 如 $6988\approx7000$, $7000$ 是 $6988$ 的近似数.

## Exercise 1

(Lesson: Numbers within 1000, Practice) Read the numbers below and say how they are composed. (1) The "Fuxing" train is about $415$ meters long. (2) Heaven Lake on Changbai Mountain is $373$ meters at its deepest, and $204$ meters deep on average. (3) The Yangtze trunk line has $111$ river-crossing bridges.

### Solution to Exercise 1

By **Definition 4.4.3 (万以内数的读法)**, $415$ is read four hundred fifteen, made of $4$ hundreds, $1$ ten, and $5$ ones; $373$ is read three hundred seventy-three; $204$ is read two hundred four (the tens place is $0$, read as one zero); $111$ is read one hundred eleven.

$\blacksquare$

## Exercise 12

(Lesson: The Abacus, Think It Over) Showing numbers on an abacus and on a counter, what is the same and what is different?

### Solution to Exercise 12

The same: both record numbers in place order, and neighboring places are decimal. Different: on a counter one bead means $1$, while on an abacus a lower bead means $1$ and an upper bead means $5$, so fewer beads are used.

$\blacksquare$
