# Assignment 20

> **Definition 3.4.1** (East, South, West, North) <br>The four basic directions. When you get up in the morning facing the sun, the front is east, the back is west, the left is north, and the right is south; you can also use a compass to tell directions.
>
> **Definition 3.4.2** (The Relative Relations of Directions) <br>East is opposite to west, and south is opposite to north. Once one direction is fixed, the other three are fixed.
>
> **Definition 3.4.3** (Directions on a Map) <br>Maps are drawn with north at the top, south at the bottom, west on the left, and east on the right; on different direction boards, the order of east, south, west, and north follows the direction the hands of a clock turn, and is fixed.

### Exercise 113

(Lesson: Recognizing East, South, West, North, Example, Campus) Walking north from the gate, you reach the teaching building. Is the teaching building on the left or the right of the playground?

### Solution to Exercise 113

By **Definition 3.4.1** (East, South, West, North) and **Definition 3.4.3** (Directions on a Map), "left" and "right" are relative to the observer; different people standing in different places have different left and right, while "east, south, west, north" are fixed. With the map's north-top, south-bottom, west-left, east-right convention, the teaching building lies to the north of the playground, rather than speaking vaguely of left or right. $\blacksquare$

### Exercise 120

(Lesson: Little Lecture Hall, Nature) What other questions about directions interest you? Pick one and investigate it. For example: facing the North Star, the front is north; the side with dense foliage is south; the side where snow melts fastest is south; the side covered with moss is north.

### Solution to Exercise 120

By **Definition 3.4.1** (East, South, West, North), when lost in the wild in the Northern Hemisphere, nature can help you tell directions: facing the North Star, the front is north; the side with dense foliage is south and the sparse side is north; the side where snow melts fast is south and the slow side is north; the side covered with moss is north and the bare side is south. Nowadays you can also use the BeiDou satellite navigation system for directions. $\blacksquare$

### Exercise 121

(Lesson: Safety Evacuation Diagram) Crowded places post safety evacuation diagrams, which help us escape quickly in an emergency such as a fire. Look at the safety evacuation diagram for the first floor of the school and point out the positions of the gate, the south gate, the west gate, the stairs, and each classroom.

### Solution to Exercise 121

By **Definition 3.4.3** (Directions on a Map), the diagram is drawn with north on top, south at the bottom, west on the left, east on the right: the top of the diagram is marked "north"; from this, the gate is at the bottom (south), the west gate is on the left (west), the south gate is at the lower right, and the stairs are on the left and right sides. In an emergency, evacuate via the nearest stairs, west gate, or south gate as the diagram indicates. $\blacksquare$
