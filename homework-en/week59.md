> **Definition 6.2.1 (口算除法)**
> 
> 整十, 整百数除以一位数, 把被除数看成几个十, 几个百再除; 两位数除以一位数, 分成整十数和个位数分别除再相加. 如 $60\div3=20$, $69\div3=23$, $600\div3=200$.
> **Definition 6.2.2 (除法估算)**
> 
> 把被除数看成与它接近的整十, 整百数再除, 只估出大约结果, 不用算出准确值. 如 $283\div3$ 在 $90$ 与 $100$ 之间.
> **Definition 6.2.3 (笔算除法法则)**
> 
> 从被除数的高位除起: 先用除数试除被除数最高位, 比除数小再试前两位; 除到哪一位, 商就写在那一位上面; 不够商 $1$ 就商 $0$ 占位; 每求出一位商, 余下的数必须比除数小.
> **Definition 6.2.4 (除法的验算)**
> 
> 没有余数时, 商$\times$除数$=$被除数; 有余数时, 商$\times$除数$+$余数$=$被除数.
> **Definition 6.2.5 (0 的除法)**
> 
> $0$ 除以任何不是 $0$ 的数都得 $0$; $0$ 不能作除数.
> **Definition 6.2.6 (用乘除法解决两步问题)**
> 
> 分析数量关系, 可从条件出发想能求什么, 也可从问题出发想先求什么, 分步列式或列综合算式. 归一问题先求单一量, 归总问题先求总量.

## Exercise 1

(Lesson: Practice 2, Problem 11) Fill in suitable numbers in the $\square$: $96\div3+\square=50$; $\left(47+19\right)\div\square=33$; $40+\square\times3=100$; $30+\square\div4=41$.

### Solution to Exercise 1

$96\div3=32$, $50-32=18$; $47+19=66$, $66\div33=2$; $100-40=60$, $60\div3=20$; $41-30=11$, $11\times4=44$. Fill in $18,2,20,44$ in order. $\blacksquare$

## Exercise 20

(Lesson: Practice 3, Problem 14) Toys are $5$ yuan and $6$ yuan; stationery is $8$ yuan and $9$ yuan. Teacher Li spent $114$ yuan on one kind of toy and $125$ yuan on one kind of stationery. (1) Which of each did she buy, and how many? (2) If this money is spent only on pencil cases, how many can be bought and how much is left?

### Solution to Exercise 20

(1) By **Definition 6.2.6 (Totalization Problem)**, divide the money spent by each unit price; the one that divides exactly is what was bought. $114\div6=19$, so the $6$-yuan toy was bought $19$ times; then divide $125$ by each stationery unit price, and the one dividing exactly gives the stationery bought and its count. (2) The total is $114+125=239$ yuan; $239\div$ pencil-case price, the quotient is the count and the remainder the money left. $\blacksquare$
