> **Definition 8.2.1 (从不同方向观察物体)**
> 
> 从前面, 上面, 左面 (或右面) 观察由小正方体搭成的物体, 看到的平面图形叫作这个方向看到的形. 从不同方向看同一个物体, 看到的图形一般不同; 从同一个位置看不同的物体, 看到的图形也可能相同.

> **Definition 8.5.4 (Triangle Inequality)**
> 
> 三角形任意两边之和大于第三边.

> **Definition 8.5.5 (三角形按角分类)**
> 
> 三角形按角可分为锐角三角形,直角三角形和钝角三角形.

> **Definition 8.5.7 (Triangle Angle Sum)**
> 
> 三角形的内角和等于 180 度.

> **Definition 8.6.1 (小数加减法)**
> 
> 计算小数减法时, 先把小数点对齐, 再按照整数减法的法则进行计算.

> **Definition 8.7.1 (轴对称图形)**
> 
> 轴对称图形中, 对称点到对称轴的距离相等.

> **Definition 8.7.2 (平移)**
> 
> 平移不改变图形的形和大小, 只改变图形的位置.

> **Definition 8.7.3 (用平移求面积和周长)**
> 
> 运用平移的方法可以将不规则图形转化为规则图形来求面积.

> **Definition 8.8.2 (复式条形统计图)**
> 
> 复式条形统计图可以同时表示多组数据, 便于直观比较.

> **Definition 8.9.1 (营养午餐的标准)**
> 
> $10$ 岁左右的儿童从每顿午餐中获取的热量应不低于 $2926$ 千焦, 脂肪应不超过 $50\,\mathrm{g}$. 搭配午餐时要同时满足热量和脂肪两项要求.

> **Definition 8.10.1 (鸡兔同笼问题与假设法)**
> 
> 解决鸡兔同笼问题时, 先假设全是鸡或全是兔, 再根据实际数量调整, 这种方法叫做假设法.

> **Definition 8.11.1 (四则运算与运算律)**
> 
> 加法交换律 a+b = b+a; 加法结合律 (a+b)+c = a+(b+c); 乘法交换律 ab = ba; 乘法结合律 (ab)c = a(bc); 乘法分配律 (a+b)c = ac+bc.

> **Definition 8.11.2 (小数意义与加减法)**
> 
> 小数的计数单位是十分之一, 百分之一, 千分之一等, 每相邻两个计数单位间的进率是 $10$. 小数加减法要把小数点对齐再计算.

> **Definition 8.11.3 (总复习: 图形与统计)**
> 
> 三角形内角和 $180^\circ$, 任意两边之和大于第三边; 轴对称图形对应点到对称轴距离相等; 平移不改变图形形大小; 复式条形统计图便于比较两组数据; 平均数 $=$ 总数 $\div$ 份数.

## Exercise 2

(Lesson: Chickens and Rabbits in a Cage, Try It) There are $10$ bicycles and tricycles altogether, with $26$ wheels in total. How many bicycles and how many tricycles are there?

### Solution to Exercise 2

By **Definition 8.10.1 (Assumption Method)**, assume all are bicycles: wheels $10\times2=20$, short by $26-20=6$; each tricycle has $1$ more wheel than a bicycle, so tricycles $6\div1=6$ and bicycles $10-6=4$.

$\blacksquare$

## Exercise 3

(Lesson: Chickens and Rabbits in a Cage, Practice 24, Problem 1) A box holds $30$ large and small steel balls weighing $266\,\mathrm{g}$ altogether. Each large ball is $11\,\mathrm{g}$ and each small ball $7\,\mathrm{g}$. How many large and how many small balls?

### Solution to Exercise 3

By **Definition 8.10.1 (Assumption Method)**, assume all are small balls: weight $30\times7=210\,\mathrm{g}$, short by $266-210=56\,\mathrm{g}$; each large ball is $11-7=4\,\mathrm{g}$ heavier, so large balls $56\div4=14$ and small balls $30-14=16$.

$\blacksquare$

## Exercise 4

(Lesson: Chickens and Rabbits in a Cage, Practice 24, Problem 2) There are $40$ tortoises and cranes altogether, with $112$ legs in total. How many tortoises and how many cranes?

### Solution to Exercise 4

By **Definition 8.10.1 (Assumption Method)**, assume all are cranes: legs $40\times2=80$, short by $112-80=32$; each tortoise has $4-2=2$ more legs than a crane, so tortoises $32\div2=16$ and cranes $40-16=24$.

$\blacksquare$

### Solution to Exercise 5

By **Definition 8.10.1 (Assumption Method)**, assume all are small boats: people $8\times4=32$, short by $38-32=6$; each large boat holds $6-4=2$ more people, so large boats $6\div2=3$ and small boats $8-3=5$.

$\blacksquare$

(Lesson: Chickens and Rabbits in a Cage, Practice 24, Problem 4) A $12$-person "environmental guardians" squad plants trees; each boy plants $3$ trees and each girl $2$ trees, planting $32$ trees altogether. How many boys and how many girls?

### Solution to Exercise 6

By **Definition 8.10.1 (Assumption Method)**, assume all are girls: trees $12\times2=24$, short by $32-24=8$; each boy plants $3-2=1$ more tree, so boys $8\div1=8$ and girls $12-8=4$.

$\blacksquare$

## Exercise 7

(Lesson: Chickens and Rabbits in a Cage, Practice 24, Problem 5) In a basketball game, a basket outside the $3$-point line scores $3$ points and inside scores $2$ points. Zhang Peng took $15$ shots, made $9$, had no free throws, and scored $21$ points. How many $3$-point baskets did he make?

### Solution to Exercise 7

By **Definition 8.10.1 (Assumption Method)**, assume all $9$ baskets were $2$-pointers: score $9\times2=18$, short by $21-18=3$; each $3$-pointer scores $1$ more than a $2$-pointer, so $3$-pointers $3\div1=3$.

$\blacksquare$

## Exercise 8

(Lesson: Chickens and Rabbits in a Cage, Practice 24, Problem 6) A knowledge quiz: $10$ points for a correct answer and $6$ points deducted for a wrong one. (1) Contestant No. $3$ answered $8$ questions and scored $64$; how many correct? (2) Contestant No. $1$ answered $10$ questions and scored $36$; how many wrong? (3) Contestant No. $2$ answered $16$ questions and scored $16$; how many correct?

### Solution to Exercise 8

By **Definition 8.10.1 (Assumption Method)**, a wrong answer loses $10+6=16$ points compared with a correct one: (1) assume all correct: $8\times10=80$, short by $80-64=16$, wrong $16\div16=1$, correct $7$; (2) assume all correct: $10\times10=100$, short by $100-36=64$, wrong $64\div16=4$; (3) assume all correct: $16\times10=160$, short by $160-16=144$, wrong $144\div16=9$, correct $16-9=7$.

$\blacksquare$

## Exercise 9

(Lesson: Chickens and Rabbits in a Cage, Practice 24, Problem 7) Teacher Li buys $6$ basketballs and volleyballs altogether for $360$ yuan. A basketball is $62$ yuan and a volleyball $58$ yuan. How many of each?

### Solution to Exercise 9

By **Definition 8.10.1 (Assumption Method)**, assume all are volleyballs: cost $6\times58=348$ yuan, short by $360-348=12$; each basketball is $62-58=4$ yuan more expensive, so basketballs $12\div4=3$ and volleyballs $6-3=3$.

$\blacksquare$

## Exercise 10

(Lesson: Chickens and Rabbits in a Cage, Practice 24, Problem 8) A hundred monks and a hundred buns: $100$ monks eat $100$ buns; each big monk eats $3$ buns, and every $3$ little monks eat $1$ bun. How many big and little monks?

### Solution to Exercise 10

By **Definition 8.10.1 (Assumption Method)**, assume all are big monks: buns $100\times3=300$, an excess of $300-100=200$. Replacing $3$ big monks with $3$ little monks changes the buns from $9$ to $1$, saving $8$. Replace $200\div8=25$ groups, each with $3$ little monks, so little monks $25\times3=75$ and big monks $100-75=25$.

$\blacksquare$
