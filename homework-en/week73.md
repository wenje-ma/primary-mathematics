> **Definition 7.4.1 (口算乘法)**
> 
> 两位数乘一位数的口算, 先把两位数分成整十数和一位数, 分别相乘后再相加.

> **Definition 7.4.2 (笔算乘法)**
> 
> 多位数乘多位数时, 把相同数位对齐, 从个位乘起, 用第二个因数的每一位分别乘第一个因数, 再把所得的积相加.

> **Definition 7.4.3 (积的变化规律)**
> 
> 一个因数不变, 另一个因数乘 (或除以) 几 (0 除外), 积也乘 (或除以) 相同的数.

> **Definition 7.4.4 (乘法估算)**
> 
> 根据问题需要把乘数估大或估小, 快速判断结果范围, 再决定精确计算.

## Exercise 1

(Lesson: Practice 7, Problem 7) Xiaoliang's essay paper holds at most $20$ characters per line, and he wrote $18$ lines; at most how many characters is the short essay?

### Solution to Exercise 1

By **Definition 7.4.1 (Mental Multiplication)**, $20\times18=360$ characters.

$\blacksquare$

## Exercise 3

(Lesson: Practice 7, Problem 9) Making one Chinese knot uses $90$ cm of cord; Aunt Wang has $10$ m of cord; is it enough to make $8$?

### Solution to Exercise 3

$10$ m $=1000$ cm, $90\times8=720$ cm, $1000>720$, enough.

$\blacksquare$

## Exercise 4

(Lesson: Practice 7, Problem 10) (1) Each bus holds at most $21$ people; at most how many do $3$ buses hold? (2) Can $80$ passengers sit in $4$ such buses?

### Solution to Exercise 4

By **Definition 7.4.1 (Mental Multiplication)**: (1) $21\times3=63$ people; (2) $21\times4=84$ people, $84>80$, they fit.

$\blacksquare$

## Exercise 5

(Lesson: Practice 7, Thinking Question) A number multiplied by $10$ is $72$ larger than the original; what is the original number?

### Solution to Exercise 5

A number times $10$ is $10$ times the original, i.e. $9$ times larger than the original. $72\div9=8$, so the original number is $8$.

$\blacksquare$
