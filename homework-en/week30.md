> **Definition 3.7.1 (7 的乘法口诀)**
> 
> 一七得七, 二七十四, 三七二十一, 四七二十八, 五七三十五, 六七四十二, 七七四十九. 相邻两句口诀的积相差 $7$; 一句口诀一般可计算两个乘法算式.
> **Definition 3.7.2 (8 的乘法口诀)**
> 
> 一八得八, 二八十六, 三八二十四, 四八三十二, 五八四十, 六八四十八, 七八五十六, 八八六十四. 相邻两句口诀的积相差 $8$.
> **Definition 3.7.3 (9 的乘法口诀)**
> 
> 一九得九, 二九十八, 三九二十七, 四九三十六, 五九四十五, 六九五十四, 七九六十三, 八九七十二, 九九八十一. 几个九就是几个十减几, 如 $9\times5$ 就是 $50-5=45$; 积的十位数字与个位数字之和都是 $9$.
> **Definition 3.7.4 (用 7~9 的乘法口诀求商)**
> 
> 计算除数是 $7,8,9$ 的除法时, 想"除数和几相乘得被除数", 那句乘法口诀中缺失的乘数就是商. 如 $56\div8$, 想"七八五十六", 商是 $7$.
> **Definition 3.7.5 (乘除两步解决问题)**
> 
> 先用乘法求出总数, 再用除法求每份数或份数; 前一步算出的结果就是后一步的条件, 两步缺一不可.
> **Definition 3.7.6 (乘法口诀表)**
> 
> 小九九口诀表共 $45$ 句, 呈三角形排列, 从"一一得一"到"九九八十一"; 除两个乘数相同的口诀外, 每句口诀可计算两个乘法算式和两个除法算式.
> **Definition 3.8.1 (乘除关系的整理)**
> 
> 加数相同的加法用乘法表示更简便, 平均分的过程用除法表示更简便; 乘法和除法互为逆运算, 同一句乘法口诀可以计算两个乘法算式和两个除法算式. 如由"二三得六"算 $2\times3=6$, $3\times2=6$, $6\div2=3$, $6\div3=2$.
> **Definition 3.8.2 (数量关系)**
> 
> 已知每份数和份数, 求总数用乘法: 每份数 $\times$ 份数 $=$ 总数; 已知总数和每份数, 求份数用除法; 已知总数和份数, 求每份数也用除法.
> **Definition 3.8.3 (方向与测量的整理)**
> 
> 认识了东, 南, 西, 北四个方向, 地图上按上北下南, 左西右东绘制; 长度单位有厘米和米, $1$ 米 $=100$ 厘米; 线段是直的, 可以量出长度; 还认识了拃, 步, 庹等身体尺.
> **Definition 3.8.4 (分类与整理的整理)**
> 
> 分类要按照一定的标准进行, 标准不同分得的结果可能不同, 但被分类事物的总数不变; 可以对第一次分类的结果按另一个标准继续分下去.

## Exercise 1

(Lesson: Organization and Review, Division Expression Table) Write all division expressions computed with the multiplication rhymes on cards, and organize them into a table in some order. (1) Say by what rule you organized them. (3) What pattern do you observe in the table? (4) Circle any cross of five expressions; what pattern do their quotients have?

### Solution to Exercise 1

Arrange by divisor (rhyme); the same rhyme corresponds to a group of divisions. Notice: in each row the divisor is unchanged, the dividend increases in turn, and the quotient increases in turn; in each column the dividend is unchanged, and the larger the divisor, the smaller the quotient. Across the cross, the quotients are equal (the same rhyme), and down the cross the divisor increases by $1$. $\blacksquare$

## Exercise 6

(Lesson: Organization and Review, Practice, Problem 5) First compute, then arrange the expressions from smallest to largest result: $54\div9$, $24-6$, $36\div4$, $7\times7$, $63\div9$, $8\times3$, $9\times8$.

## Exercise 7

### Solution to Exercise 7

By **Definition 3.7.5 (Two-Step Multiplication-Division Problems)**: each group gets $24\div3=8$ (books); each person gets $8\div4=2$ (books). $\blacksquare$

## Exercise 8

(Lesson: Organization and Review, Problem 7) Price relations of a rubber ball, a soccer ball, and a basketball: the soccer ball costs $2$ times the rubber ball, and the basketball costs $2$ times the soccer ball. A sum of money exactly buys $8$ soccer balls. If spent on rubber balls, how many can be bought? On basketballs?

### Solution to Exercise 8

Let the rubber ball be $1$ share, the soccer ball $2$ shares, the basketball $4$ shares. The money for $8$ soccer balls is $8\times2=16$ shares; rubber balls: $16\div1=16$ (balls); basketballs: $16\div4=4$ (balls). $\blacksquare$

## Exercise 9

(Lesson: Organization and Review, Problem 8) A division problem has divisor $6$. Xiaoming swapped the tens digit and ones digit of the dividend, and got a quotient of $4$. What was the dividend? What is the correct quotient?

### Solution to Exercise 9

The misread dividend: $6\times4=24$. Swapping the tens and ones digits of $24$ back gives $42$. The correct dividend is $42$, and the correct quotient: $42\div6=7$. $\blacksquare$

### Solution to Exercise 12
