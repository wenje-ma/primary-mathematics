> **Definition 3.2.3 (乘法口诀)**
> 
> 乘法口诀是计算表内乘法的基础, 每句口诀对应两个乘法算式 (如二三得六对应 2*3=6 和 3*2=6).
> **Definition 3.3.1 (平均分)**
> 
> 每份分得同样多, 叫平均分. 如把 $6$ 块糖分成 $3$ 份,每份 $2$ 块, 就是平均分.
> **Definition 3.3.2 (除法的含义)**
> 
> 平均分的过程可以用除法表示. 如 $12$ 个竹笋每 $4$ 个放一盘, 能放 $3$ 盘, 写成 $12\div4=3$, 读作"$12$ 除以 $4$ 等于 $3$".
> **Definition 3.3.3 (除法算式各部分名称)**
> 
> 除号前面的数叫被除数, 除号后面的数叫除数, 除得的结果叫商. 如在 $12\div4=3$ 中, $12$ 是被除数, $4$ 是除数, $3$ 是商.
> **Definition 3.3.4 (用乘法口诀求商)**
> 
> 计算除法时, 想"除数和几相乘得被除数", 那句乘法口诀的得数就是商. 如 $12\div3$, 想"三 (四) 十二", 商是 $4$.
> **Definition 3.3.5 (乘除关系)**
> 
> 乘法和除法有关系, 可以用同一句乘法口诀计算. 如由"三四十二"可算 $3\times4=12$, $4\times3=12$, $12\div3=4$, $12\div4=3$.
> **Definition 3.4.1 (东,南,西,北)**
> 
> 四个基本方向. 早晨起来面向太阳, 前面是东, 后面是西, 左面是北, 右面是南; 也可以用指南针辨别方向.
> **Definition 3.4.2 (方向的相对关系)**
> 
> 东和西相对, 南和北相对. 只要确定一个方向, 其他三个方向就确定了.
> **Definition 3.4.3 (地图上的方向)**
> 
> 地图是按上北,下南,左西,右东的方式绘制的; 在不同的方向上, 东,南,西,北的排列顺序与时针转动方向一致, 是不变的.

## Exercise 1

(Lesson: Practice, Problem 7) Draw a picture and use it to explain the meaning of the expressions $3\times4=12$, $4\times3=12$, $12\div3=4$, $12\div4=3$.

### Solution to Exercise 1

By **Definition 3.3.5 (The Relation Between Multiplication and Division)**, draw $3$ plates with $4$ apples each: $3\times4=12$ finds the total; $4\times3=12$ swaps the factors; $12\div3=4$ finds the number per plate; $12\div4=3$ finds the number of plates. The four expressions use the same rhyme "three four is twelve". $\blacksquare$

## Exercise 9

(Lesson: Practice, Problem 7) (1) Each vase holds $5$ peonies; how many peonies do $4$ vases hold? (2) $10$ peonies arranged equally in $2$ vases; how many per vase? (3) $10$ peonies arranged in $2$ vases, one vase holds $6$; how many does the other hold?

### Solution to Exercise 9

(1) find $4$ copies of $5$ stems: $5\times4=20$ (stems); (2) divide $10$ stems equally among $2$ vases: $10\div2=5$ (stems); (3) remove $6$ stems from $10$: $10-6=4$ (stems). $\blacksquare$

## Exercise 12

(Lesson: Recognizing East, South, West, North, Example, Campus) Walking north from the gate, you reach the teaching building. Is the teaching building on the left or the right of the playground?

### Solution to Exercise 12

By **Definition 3.4.1 (East, South, West, North)** and **Definition 3.4.3 (Directions on a Map)**, "left" and "right" are relative to the observer; different people standing in different places have different left and right, while "east, south, west, north" are fixed. With the map's north-top, south-bottom, west-left, east-right convention, the teaching building lies to the north of the playground, rather than speaking vaguely of left or right. $\blacksquare$

### Solution to Exercise 13
