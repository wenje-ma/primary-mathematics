> **Definition 8.1.1 (加法的意义和各部分间的关系)**
> 
> 把两个数合并成一个数的运算, 叫作加法. 相加的两个数叫作加数, 加得的数叫作和. 和 $=$ 加数 $+$ 加数, 加数 $=$ 和 $-$ 另一个加数.
> **Definition 8.1.2 (减法的意义和各部分间的关系)**
> 
> 已知两个加数的和与其中一个加数, 求另一个加数的运算, 叫作减法. 在减法中, 已知的和叫作被减数. 减法是加法的逆运算. 差 $=$ 被减数 $-$ 减数, 被减数 $=$ 减数 $+$ 差, 减数 $=$ 被减数 $-$ 差.
> **Definition 8.1.3 (乘法的意义和各部分间的关系)**
> 
> 求几个相同加数的和的简便运算, 叫作乘法. 相乘的两个数叫作因数, 乘得的数叫作积. 积 $=$ 因数 $\times$ 因数, 因数 $=$ 积 $\div$ 另一个因数.
> **Definition 8.1.4 (除法的意义和各部分间的关系)**
> 
> 已知两个因数的积与其中一个因数, 求另一个因数的运算, 叫作除法. 在除法中, 已知的积叫作被除数. 除法是乘法的逆运算. 商 $=$ 被除数 $\div$ 除数, 除数 $=$ 被除数 $\div$ 商, 被除数 $=$ 商 $\times$ 除数.
> **Definition 8.1.5 (有关 0 的运算)**
> 
> 一个数加上 $0$ 还得原数; 被减数等于减数时, 差是 $0$; 一个数和 $0$ 相乘仍得 $0$; $0$ 除以一个非 $0$ 的数还得 $0$. $0$ 不能作除数.
> **Definition 8.1.6 (有余数除法各部分间的关系)**
> 
> 在有余数的除法里, 被除数 $=$ 商 $\times$ 除数 $+$ 余数, 余数要比除数小.
> **Definition 8.1.7 (四则混合运算的顺序)**
> 
> 加, 减, 乘, 除四种运算统称四则运算. 一个算式里, 既有小括号又有中括号, 要先算小括号里面的, 再算中括号里面的, 最后算括号外面的.
> **Definition 8.1.8 (租船租车问题的策略)**
> 
> 解决怎样租船 (租车) 最省钱的问题, 先算每种船 (车) 每个座位的单价, 优先租单价便宜的, 再调整尽量不空座位, 比较几种方案后选最便宜的.

## Exercise 2

(Lesson: Meaning of Multiplication and Division and Their Parts, Try It) Based on $36\times14=504$, write the answers to the following two directly: $504\div14=$ ___; $504\div36=$ ___.

### Solution to Exercise 2

By **Definition 8.1.4 (Meaning of Division and the Relationships Among Its Parts)**, division is the inverse of multiplication; dividing the product by one factor gives the other factor: $504\div14=36$, $504\div36=14$. $\blacksquare$

## Exercise 3

(Lesson: Meaning of Multiplication and Division and Their Parts, Practice 2, Problem 1) Which method should be used for the following, and why? (1) A snail crawls $5\,\mathrm{m}$ per hour. How many meters can it crawl in $6$ hours? (2) A snail crawled $30\,\mathrm{m}$ in $6$ hours. How many meters does it crawl per hour on average? (3) With $120$ pencils, $12$ per box, how many boxes can be filled? (4) An elephant weighs $5600\,\mathrm{kg}$, exactly $8$ times the weight of a cow. How many kilograms does this cow weigh?

### Solution to Exercise 3

(1) By **Definition 8.1.3 (Multiplication)**, finding what $6$ copies of $5$ is uses multiplication: $5\times6=30$ ($\mathrm{m}$). (2) By **Definition 8.1.4 (Division)**, equal sharing uses division: $30\div6=5$ ($\mathrm{m}$). (3) $120\div12=10$ (boxes). (4) Given that $8$ times a number is $5600$, find the number with division: $5600\div8=700$ ($\mathrm{kg}$). $\blacksquare$

## Exercise 4

(Lesson: Meaning of Multiplication and Division and Their Parts, Practice 2, Problem 2) Based on the relationships among the parts of multiplication and division, write the other two expressions: $17\times42=714$; $208\times67=13936$; $1008\div48=21$; $1125\div25=45$.

### Solution to Exercise 4

By **Definition 8.1.3 (Multiplication)** and **Definition 8.1.4 (Division)**, which are inverse operations: $17\times42=714 \to 714\div17=42,\ 714\div42=17$; $208\times67=13936 \to 13936\div208=67,\ 13936\div67=208$; $1008\div48=21 \to 1008\div21=48,\ 21\times48=1008$; $1125\div25=45 \to 1125\div45=25,\ 45\times25=1125$. $\blacksquare$

## Exercise 7

(Lesson: Meaning of Multiplication and Division and Their Parts, Practice 2, Problem 5) A pile of peaches is divided among $6$ little monkeys; each monkey gets $12$ peaches, and $3$ peaches remain. How many peaches are in the pile altogether?

### Solution to Exercise 7

## Exercise 9

(Lesson: Operations Involving 0, Practice 2, Problem 7) A spacecraft travels $55\,\mathrm{km}$ in $5$ seconds. Based on this data, fill in the following table: time $3$ seconds and $13$ seconds correspond to the distances; distances $77\,\mathrm{km}$ and $176\,\mathrm{km}$ correspond to the times.

### Solution to Exercise 9

The spacecraft's speed is $55\div5=11$ ($\mathrm{km/s}$). Time $3$ seconds gives distance $11\times3=33\,\mathrm{km}$; time $13$ seconds gives distance $11\times13=143\,\mathrm{km}$; distance $77\,\mathrm{km}$ gives time $77\div11=7$ seconds; distance $176\,\mathrm{km}$ gives time $176\div11=16$ seconds. $\blacksquare$

### Solution to Exercise 10

## Exercise 12

(Lesson: Meaning of Multiplication and Division and Their Parts, Practice 2, Problem 10) Given $\triangle+\square=\bigcirc$ (the sum of two figures) and $▲\times\diamond=\bigstar$ (the product of two figures), which of the following expressions are correct? Mark correct ones with "√" and wrong ones with "×": $\bigcirc-\triangle=\square$; $\bigcirc-\square=\triangle$; $\bigstar\div▲=\diamond$; $\bigstar\div\diamond=▲$.

### Solution to Exercise 12

By **Definition 8.1.2 (Subtraction is the inverse of addition)**, subtracting one addend from the sum gives the other addend, so $\bigcirc-\triangle=\square$ and $\bigcirc-\square=\triangle$ are both correct. By **Definition 8.1.4 (Division is the inverse of multiplication)**, dividing the product by one factor gives the other factor, so $\bigstar\div▲=\diamond$ and $\bigstar\div\diamond=▲$ are both correct. $\blacksquare$
