> **Definition 3.7.1 (7 的乘法口诀)**
> 
> 一七得七, 二七十四, 三七二十一, 四七二十八, 五七三十五, 六七四十二, 七七四十九. 相邻两句口诀的积相差 $7$; 一句口诀一般可计算两个乘法算式.
> **Definition 3.7.2 (8 的乘法口诀)**
> 
> 一八得八, 二八十六, 三八二十四, 四八三十二, 五八四十, 六八四十八, 七八五十六, 八八六十四. 相邻两句口诀的积相差 $8$.
> **Definition 3.7.3 (9 的乘法口诀)**
> 
> 一九得九, 二九十八, 三九二十七, 四九三十六, 五九四十五, 六九五十四, 七九六十三, 八九七十二, 九九八十一. 几个九就是几个十减几, 如 $9\times5$ 就是 $50-5=45$; 积的十位数字与个位数字之和都是 $9$.
> **Definition 3.7.4 (用 7~9 的乘法口诀求商)**
> 
> 计算除数是 $7,8,9$ 的除法时, 想"除数和几相乘得被除数", 那句乘法口诀中缺失的乘数就是商. 如 $56\div8$, 想"七八五十六", 商是 $7$.
> **Definition 3.7.5 (乘除两步解决问题)**
> 
> 先用乘法求出总数, 再用除法求每份数或份数; 前一步算出的结果就是后一步的条件, 两步缺一不可.
> **Definition 3.7.6 (乘法口诀表)**
> 
> 小九九口诀表共 $45$ 句, 呈三角形排列, 从"一一得一"到"九九八十一"; 除两个乘数相同的口诀外, 每句口诀可计算两个乘法算式和两个除法算式.

## Exercise 1

(Lesson: Multiplication Rhymes of 8, Practice, Problem 4) The results within each group are equal; what goes in the $\square$? What do you notice? $2\times6+6=\square\times6$, $4\times6-6=\square\times6$, $4\times7+7=\square\times7$, $\square\times7-7=\square\times7$, $8\times\square+8=\square\times8$, $8\times8-8=\square\times8$.

### Solution to Exercise 1

$2\times6+6$ is $2$ copies of $6$ plus $1$ copy of $6$, equal to $3$ copies of $6$, fill in $3$; $4\times6-6$ is $4$ copies of $6$ minus $1$ copy of $6$, equal to $3$ copies of $6$, fill in $3$; $4\times7+7=5\times7$; $5\times7-7=4\times7$; $8\times7+8=8\times8$; $8\times8-8=8\times7$. Notice: adding or subtracting one of a number means one more or one fewer of that number, converting to so-many copies. $\blacksquare$

## Exercise 3

(Lesson: Multiplication Rhymes of 9, Example, Winter-Solving Picture) Starting from the Winter Solstice, each $1$ day paint $1$ petal of a plum blossom red; finishing $1$ blossom is $1$ "nine", and when $9$ blossoms are painted, spring arrives. Make up the rhymes of $9$: from $9\times1=9$ up to $9\times9=81$. What do you notice about the products?

### Solution to Exercise 3

By **Definition 3.7.3 (Multiplication Rhymes of 9)**: one nine is nine, two nine eighteen, three nine twenty-seven, four nine thirty-six, five nine forty-five, six nine fifty-four, seven nine sixty-three, eight nine seventy-two, nine nine eighty-one. Notice: each later product is $9$ more than the previous; the ones digit decreases by $1$ each time while the tens digit increases by $1$; how many nines is how many tens minus that many, for example $9\times5=50-5=45$. $\blacksquare$

## Exercise 5

(Lesson: Multiplication Rhymes of 9, Math Game) Use both hands to show the rhymes of $9$: the fingers to the left of the bent finger show the tens digit, and the fingers to the right show the ones digit. Can you show the other rhymes with your fingers?

### Solution to Exercise 5

For example "three nine twenty-seven": bend the $3$rd finger from the left, there are $2$ fingers on the left (tens $2$) and $7$ fingers on the right (ones $7$), giving $27$. The idea: bend the $n$th finger, the $n-1$ fingers on the left show the tens and the $10-n$ fingers on the right show the ones, together making $10\left(n-1\right)+\left(10-n\right)=9n$. $\blacksquare$

## Exercise 15

(Lesson: Finding the Quotient with the Rhymes of 7~9, Do It, Problem 1) From the picture say the meaning of these expressions: $4\times8=32$, $32\div8=4$, $32\div4=8$. What is the same about each group?

### Solution to Exercise 15

$4\times8=32$ means $8$ copies of $4$ make $32$; $32\div8=4$ means dividing $32$ equally into $8$ shares of $4$ each; $32\div4=8$ means $32$ contains $8$ copies of $4$. The three expressions use the same rhyme "four eight thirty-two", and multiplication and division are inverse operations. $\blacksquare$
