> **Definition 3.2.1 (乘法的含义)**
> 
> 求几个相同加数的和, 可以用乘法表示. 如 $5$ 个 $3$ 相加, 写成 $3\times5=15$, 读作"$3$ 乘 $5$ 等于 $15$".

> **Definition 3.2.2 (乘法算式各部分名称)**
> 
> 乘号两边的数都叫乘数, 乘得的结果叫积. 如在 $3\times5=15$ 中, $3$ 和 $5$ 是乘数, $15$ 是积.

> **Definition 3.2.3 (乘法口诀)**
> 
> 乘法口诀是计算表内乘法的基础, 每句口诀对应两个乘法算式 (如二三得六对应 2*3=6 和 3*2=6).

> **Definition 3.2.4 (乘加,乘减)**
> 
> 形如 $3\times3+2$,$3\times4-1$ 的算式叫乘加,乘减; 在这样的算式中, 要先算乘法, 再算加法或减法.

> **Definition 3.2.5 (乘法解决问题)**
> 
> 求几个几是多少, 用乘法计算; 把相同加数连加的问题, 都可以用乘法简便地解决.

## Exercise 7

(Lesson: Practice, Problem 2) $6\times3+6=\_\_$, $6\times4+6=\_\_$, $6\times2+6=\_\_$; $6\times6-6=\_\_$, $6\times5-6=\_\_$, $6\times4-6=\_\_$. What do you notice?

### Solution to Exercise 7

By **Definition 3.2.4 (Multiplication-Addition and Multiplication-Subtraction)**, each problem progresses between the rhymes of $6$: $6\times3+6=6\times4=24$, $6\times4+6=6\times5=30$, $6\times2+6=6\times3=18$; $6\times6-6=6\times5=30$, $6\times5-6=6\times4=24$, $6\times4-6=6\times3=18$. Notice that between two adjacent rhymes of $6$, the products differ by $6$. $\blacksquare$

## Exercise 12

(Lesson: Solving Problems, Example, Tables) Compare the two problems below and choose the appropriate method to solve them. (1) There are $4$ rows of tables, $5$ tables in each row; how many tables are there in total? (2) There are two rows of tables, one row has $4$ tables and the other row has $5$ tables; how many tables are there in total?

### Solution to Exercise 12

By **Definition 3.2.5 (Solving Problems with Multiplication)**, (1) is $4$ copies of $5$ added, use multiplication: $5\times4=20$ (tables); (2) is combining $4$ and $5$, use addition: $4+5=9$ (tables). Both problems involve $4$ and $5$, but (1) is "several groups of a number" while (2) is combining two parts, so the methods are different. $\blacksquare$
