> **Definition 4.5.1 (万以内加法笔算)**
> 
> 列竖式加法时, 相同数位对齐, 从个位加起; 哪一位上的数相加满十, 就向前一位进 $1$. 相同数位对齐是为了相同计数单位的个数相加.

> **Definition 4.5.2 (万以内减法笔算)**
> 
> 列竖式减法时, 相同数位对齐, 从个位减起; 哪一位上的数不够减, 就从前一位退 $1$, 在本位上加 $10$ 再减.

> **Definition 4.5.3 (加法各部分间的关系)**
> 
> 把两个数合并成一个数的运算叫加法. 和 $=$ 加数 $+$ 加数, 加数 $=$ 和 $-$ 另一个加数.

> **Definition 4.5.4 (减法各部分间的关系)**
> 
> 已知两个加数的和与其中一个加数, 求另一个加数的运算叫减法, 减法是加法的逆运算. 差 $=$ 被减数 $-$ 减数, 减数 $=$ 被减数 $-$ 差, 被减数 $=$ 减数 $+$ 差.

> **Definition 4.6.1 (数学连环画)**
> 
> 用几幅按顺序连起来的图画, 讲述一个包含数学信息或数量关系的故事, 每幅图配文字说明, 并能从中提出数学问题. 如画中要标出每幅画的顺序.

> **Definition 4.6.2 (连环画中的除法情境)**
> 
> 把连环画故事里的数量关系用算式表示. 如买了 $11$ 个罐头, 每天吃 $5$ 个, $11\div5=2$(天)……$1$(个), 表示够吃 $2$ 天, 还剩 $1$ 个.

## Exercise 1

(Lesson: Estimation, Do It) The cinema has $500$ seats; Grades 1–3 bring $298$ people and Grades 4–6 bring $187$ people; can all six grades sit together?

### Solution to Exercise 1

Estimate: $298\approx300$, $187\approx200$, $300+200=500$; actually $298< 300$, $187< 200$, so the actual sum is less than $500$, and they can sit. $\blacksquare$

## Exercise 2

(Lesson: Solving Problems, Practice) Which method do you use for each problem below, and why? (1) The ski resort sold $86$ tickets in the morning and $59$ in the afternoon; how many in all? (2) $145$ tickets in all, $86$ in the morning; how many in the afternoon? (3) $843$ people in the whole school, $418$ boys; how many girls?

### Solution to Exercise 2

(1) Finding the total uses addition: $86+59=145$. (2) Knowing the sum and one addend to find the other uses subtraction: $145-86=59$. (3) Knowing the total and the boys to find the girls uses subtraction: $843-418=425$. $\blacksquare$

## Exercise 3

(Lesson: Solving Problems, Practice) From the relations among the parts of addition and subtraction, write the other two expressions: $28+19=47$; $203+147=350$; $67-55=12$; $850-239=611$.

### Solution to Exercise 3

$28+19=47\Rightarrow47-19=28$, $47-28=19$; $203+147=350\Rightarrow350-147=203$, $350-203=147$; $67-55=12\Rightarrow67-12=55$, $55+12=67$; $850-239=611\Rightarrow850-611=239$, $611+239=850$. $\blacksquare$

## Exercise 4

(Lesson: Solving Problems, Practice) Fill in the number each $\square$ stands for: $\square+18=37$; $\square-73=100$; $540-\square=210$; $150+\square=210$; $55+\square=80$; $200+\square=360$.

### Solution to Exercise 4

By **Definition 4.5.3 (Addend = Sum - Other Addend)** and **Definition 4.5.4 (减法各部分间的关系)**: $\square=37-18=19$; $\square=100+73=173$; $\square=540-210=330$; $\square=210-150=60$; $\square=80-55=25$; $\square=360-200=160$. $\blacksquare$

## Exercise 5

(Lesson: Solving Problems, Practice) A book has $237$ pages, and Xiaoli read $143$; is "the rest is less than $100$ pages" correct?

### Solution to Exercise 5

The rest is $237-143=94$ pages, and $94< 100$, so "the rest is less than $100$ pages" is correct. $\blacksquare$

## Exercise 6

### Solution to Exercise 6

## Exercise 7

(Lesson: Solving Problems, Practice) Museum visits: morning $420$ people, afternoon $384$, whole day $804$. From the expressions, choose the matching condition and question by number: $420+384=804$(people); $804-420=384$(people); $804-384=420$(people).

### Solution to Exercise 7

$420+384=804$ picks ①morning $420$, ②afternoon $384$, question C how many in all; $804-420=384$ picks ③whole day, ①morning, question B how many in the afternoon; $804-384=420$ picks ③whole day, ②afternoon, question A how many in the morning. $\blacksquare$

## Exercise 8

(Lesson: Solving Problems, Practice) A cold-drink shop sold $219$ popsicles in the morning and $402$ in the afternoon. (1) Xiaojun says the morning and afternoon together sold less than $600$; is he right? (2) Raise other questions and solve them.

### Solution to Exercise 8

(1) $219+402=621$ popsicles, and $621> 600$, so Xiaojun is wrong. (2) For example the afternoon sold $402-219=183$ more than the morning. The answer is not unique. $\blacksquare$

## Exercise 9

(Lesson: Solving Problems, Practice) Knowing that the sum of two numbers is $369$ more than one addend and $485$ more than the other addend, what is the sum of the two addends?

### Solution to Exercise 9

What the sum exceeds one addend by is exactly the other addend, so the two addends are $369$ and $485$, and the sum is $369+485=854$. $\blacksquare$

## Exercise 10

(Lesson: Sudoku, Try It) Fill a $4\times4$ grid with $1\sim4$ so that every row, column, and $2\times2$ box uses $1\sim4$ without repetition; how do you continue?

### Solution to Exercise 10

First look at which empty cell's row, column, or box already has the most filled numbers, and fill the only number possible. For example column 1 already has $1$ and $3$, row 2 has $4$, so column 1 row 2 can only take $2$, then eliminate box by box. $\blacksquare$

## Exercise 16

(Lesson: Organization and Review, Practice) An online shop takes $279$ orders by day and $405$ at night; one order needs one shipping box; are $600$ boxes enough? If not, how many short?

### Solution to Exercise 16

In all $279+405=684$ orders, and $684> 600$, so not enough; short by $684-600=84$ boxes. $\blacksquare$

## Exercise 18

(Lesson: Organization and Review, Practice) When Xiaoyu added two three-digit numbers, he mistook the $6$ in one addend's tens place for $8$, and the $5$ in the other addend's hundreds place for $3$; he got $569$. What is the correct result?

### Solution to Exercise 18

The tens $6$ mistaken for $8$ over-counted by $20$, and the hundreds $5$ mistaken for $3$ under-counted by $200$; in all under-counted by $200-20=180$, so the correct result is $569+180=749$. $\blacksquare$

## Exercise 19

(Lesson: Picture-Strip Sharing) Xiaodong's strip "The Kitten Buys Cans": the kitten family goes to the supermarket, buys $11$ cans and goes home happily, and they eat $5$ cans each day. What math knowledge does this strip contain? Eating $5$ cans a day, for how many days is it enough? How many cans will the kitten family eat in $2$ days?

### Solution to Exercise 19

By **Definition 4.6.2 (Division Situations in the Picture-Strip)**, $11\div5=2$(days)……$1$(can), enough for $2$ days, with $1$ can left; in $2$ days they eat $5\times2=10$ cans. The strip contains division with remainder and multiplication. $\blacksquare$
