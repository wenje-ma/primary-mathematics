> **Definition 3.2.3 (乘法口诀)**
> 
> 乘法口诀是计算表内乘法的基础, 每句口诀对应两个乘法算式 (如二三得六对应 2*3=6 和 3*2=6).

> **Definition 3.3.1 (平均分)**
> 
> 每份分得同样多, 叫平均分. 如把 $6$ 块糖分成 $3$ 份,每份 $2$ 块, 就是平均分.

> **Definition 3.3.2 (除法的含义)**
> 
> 平均分的过程可以用除法表示. 如 $12$ 个竹笋每 $4$ 个放一盘, 能放 $3$ 盘, 写成 $12\div4=3$, 读作"$12$ 除以 $4$ 等于 $3$".

> **Definition 3.3.3 (除法算式各部分名称)**
> 
> 除号前面的数叫被除数, 除号后面的数叫除数, 除得的结果叫商. 如在 $12\div4=3$ 中, $12$ 是被除数, $4$ 是除数, $3$ 是商.

> **Definition 3.3.4 (用乘法口诀求商)**
> 
> 计算除法时, 想"除数和几相乘得被除数", 那句乘法口诀的得数就是商. 如 $12\div3$, 想"三 (四) 十二", 商是 $4$.

> **Definition 3.3.5 (乘除关系)**
> 
> 乘法和除法有关系, 可以用同一句乘法口诀计算. 如由"三四十二"可算 $3\times4=12$, $4\times3=12$, $12\div3=4$, $12\div4=3$.

## Exercise 10

(Lesson: Solving Problems, Example, Water Cups) Spending $15$ yuan on $3$ identical water cups, how much is each? One water cup is $5$ yuan; with $15$ yuan how many cups can you buy?

### Solution to Exercise 10

By **Definition 3.3.2 (The Meaning of Division)**, the first problem is "divide $15$ yuan equally into $3$ shares": $15\div3=5$ (yuan), so each is $5$ yuan; the second is "find how many copies of $5$ yuan are in $15$ yuan": $15\div5=3$ (cups), so you can buy $3$. Both use division, but from different angles. $\blacksquare$

## Exercise 11

(Lesson: Do It, Socks) There are two packs of socks: one has $5$ pairs per pack priced at $20$ yuan; the other has $6$ pairs per pack priced at $30$ yuan. Which pack has the cheaper price per pair?

### Solution to Exercise 11

By **Definition 3.3.2 (The Meaning of Division)**, first compute the price per pair: the first is $20\div5=4$ (yuan), the second is $30\div6=5$ (yuan); since $4<5$, the pack of $5$ pairs priced at $20$ yuan is cheaper. $\blacksquare$

## Exercise 13

(Lesson: Practice, Problem 2) Look at the picture and explain the meaning of the expressions. $5\times6=30$, $30\div6=5$, $30\div5=6$.

### Solution to Exercise 13

By **Definition 3.3.5 (The Relation Between Multiplication and Division)**, $5\times6=30$ means $6$ copies of $5$ add to $30$; $30\div6=5$ means dividing $30$ equally into $6$ shares of $5$ each; $30\div5=6$ means $30$ contains $6$ copies of $5$. The three expressions use the same rhyme "five six thirty". $\blacksquare$

### Solution to Exercise 18
