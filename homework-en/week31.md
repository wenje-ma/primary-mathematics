> **Definition 3.8.1 (乘除关系的整理)**
> 
> 加数相同的加法用乘法表示更简便, 平均分的过程用除法表示更简便; 乘法和除法互为逆运算, 同一句乘法口诀可以计算两个乘法算式和两个除法算式. 如由"二三得六"算 $2\times3=6$, $3\times2=6$, $6\div2=3$, $6\div3=2$.

> **Definition 3.8.2 (数量关系)**
> 
> 已知每份数和份数, 求总数用乘法: 每份数 $\times$ 份数 $=$ 总数; 已知总数和每份数, 求份数用除法; 已知总数和份数, 求每份数也用除法.

> **Definition 3.8.3 (方向与测量的整理)**
> 
> 认识了东, 南, 西, 北四个方向, 地图上按上北下南, 左西右东绘制; 长度单位有厘米和米, $1$ 米 $=100$ 厘米; 线段是直的, 可以量出长度; 还认识了拃, 步, 庹等身体尺.

> **Definition 3.8.4 (分类与整理的整理)**
> 
> 分类要按照一定的标准进行, 标准不同分得的结果可能不同, 但被分类事物的总数不变; 可以对第一次分类的结果按另一个标准继续分下去.

## Exercise 1

(Lesson: Applying and Improving, Problem 3, Classification) Cards are marked $1,2,3,\cdots,20$. (1) Set your own standard and divide the cards into two classes. (2) Can the result of the classification be divided further? Discuss with classmates.

### Solution to Exercise 1

By **Definition 3.8.4 (Reviewing Classification and Organization)**, for example divide by odd and even: the odds $1,3,5,\cdots,19$ are $10$ cards, and the evens $2,4,6,\cdots,20$ are $10$ cards. (2) It can be divided further, for example among the evens by whether they are multiples of $5$, or by one-digit and two-digit. Different standards give different results, but the total is always $20$. $\blacksquare$

## Exercise 2

(Lesson: Practice, Problem 1, Scent Bags) (1) There are $8$ scent bags per row and $3$ rows; how many in total? (2) $24$ scent bags are strung equally into $6$ strings; how many per string? (3) $24$ scent bags, $4$ per string; how many strings can be made?

### Solution to Exercise 2

By **Definition 3.8.2 (Quantity Relations)**: (1) $8\times3=24$ (bags). (2) $24\div6=4$ (bags). (3) $24\div4=6$ (strings). $\blacksquare$

## Exercise 8

(Lesson: Practice, Problem 7, Knife and Pencil) Fill in from the picture: the knife is ( ) centimeters long, the pencil is ( ) centimeters long. (1) How many centimeters shorter is the knife than the pencil? (2) How many centimeters longer is the pencil than the knife?

### Solution to Exercise 8

Read the knife's and pencil's lengths from the marks in the picture. (1) How much shorter the knife is: pencil length $-$ knife length. (2) How much longer the pencil is: pencil length $-$ knife length; the two answers are the same. For example if the pencil is $10$ centimeters and the knife is $6$, then $10-6=4$ centimeters. $\blacksquare$

## Exercise 11

(Lesson: Practice, Problem 10, Chickens and Ducks) Grandma Liu raises two different kinds of chickens, one kind has $3$ and the other has $6$. She also raises $3$ kinds of ducks, each kind has $6$. (1) How many chickens does Grandma Liu raise? (2) How many ducks? (3) Raise other math problems and solve them.

### Solution to Exercise 11

(1) The chickens are two different kinds, use addition: $3+6=9$ (chickens). (2) The ducks are $3$ kinds of $6$ each, use multiplication: $3\times6=18$ (ducks). (3) For example how many chickens and ducks altogether? $9+18=27$ (birds); how many more ducks than chickens? $18-9=9$ (birds). $\blacksquare$

## Exercise 12

(Lesson: Practice, Problem 11, Pose a Problem) From the conditions and expressions, pose the problem: Xiaohong brought $24$ yuan, exactly buying $3$ identical small cakes. (1) $24\div3=8$ (yuan), the problem: ? (2) $8\times9=72$ (yuan), the problem: ?

### Solution to Exercise 12

(1) $24\div3=8$ finds the price of each small cake; problem: "how much is each small cake?" (2) From (1) each cake is $8$ yuan, and $8\times9=72$ finds the total price of $9$ cakes; problem: "how much for $9$ such small cakes?" $\blacksquare$

## Exercise 13

(Lesson: Practice, Problem 12, Sports Equipment) Class 2(1) buys sports gear: socks come in three kinds (the first $8$ yuan for $2$ pairs, the second $20$ yuan for $4$ pairs, the third $3$ yuan for $1$ pair); a rubber ball is $5$ yuan each, a shuttlecock is $3$ yuan each. (1) Aunt Wang buys $4$ pairs of socks; which kind is cheapest? (2) How much for $5$ rubber balls? (3) The money for $9$ shuttlecocks exactly buys one soccer ball; how much are $9$ shuttlecocks? How much is one soccer ball?

### Solution to Exercise 13

(1) The first kind is $8\div2=4$ yuan per pair, the second is $20\div4=5$ yuan per pair, the third is $3$ yuan per pair, so the third kind is cheapest. (2) $5\times5=25$ (yuan). (3) $9\times3=27$ (yuan), i.e. $9$ shuttlecocks are $27$ yuan, and one soccer ball is $27$ yuan. $\blacksquare$
