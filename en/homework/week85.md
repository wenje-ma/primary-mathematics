> **Definition 10.8.1 (Adding Like Fractions)**<br>同分母分数相加减, 分母不变, 只把分子相加减.
> **Definition 10.8.2 (Adding Unlike Fractions)**<br>异分母分数相加减, 先通分, 再按照同分母分数加减法的法则计算.
> **Definition 10.8.3 (分数加减混合运算)**<br>分数加减混合运算的运算顺序与整数相同: 没有括号的从左往右算, 有括号的先算括号里面的. 整数加法的交换律, 结合律对分数加法同样适用.

## Exercise 129

(Lesson: Like-Denominator Addition/Subtraction, Do It) Compute: $\frac{2}{9}+\frac{5}{9}$, $\frac{6}{7}-\frac{2}{7}$, $\frac{7}{10}-\frac{1}{10}$, $\frac{5}{8}+\frac{1}{8}$.

### Solution to Exercise 129

By **Definition 10.8.1 (Adding/Subtracting Fractions with Like Denominators)**: $\frac{2}{9}+\frac{5}{9}=\frac{7}{9}$; $\frac{6}{7}-\frac{2}{7}=\frac{4}{7}$; $\frac{7}{10}-\frac{1}{10}=\frac{6}{10}=\frac{3}{5}$; $\frac{5}{8}+\frac{1}{8}=\frac{6}{8}=\frac{3}{4}$. $\blacksquare$

## Exercise 130

(Lesson: Like-Denominator Addition/Subtraction, Exercise 23, Problem 1) Compute: $\frac{5}{12}+\frac{1}{12}$, $\frac{7}{8}-\frac{3}{8}$, $\frac{3}{10}+\frac{9}{10}$, $\frac{11}{15}-\frac{11}{15}$.

### Solution to Exercise 130

By **Definition 10.8.1 (Adding/Subtracting Fractions with Like Denominators)**: $\frac{5}{12}+\frac{1}{12}=\frac{6}{12}=\frac{1}{2}$; $\frac{7}{8}-\frac{3}{8}=\frac{4}{8}=\frac{1}{2}$; $\frac{3}{10}+\frac{9}{10}=\frac{12}{10}=\frac{6}{5}$; $\frac{11}{15}-\frac{11}{15}=0$. $\blacksquare$

## Exercise 131

(Lesson: Like-Denominator Addition/Subtraction, Exercise 23, Problem 5) The share of students in each grade of a primary school is: Grade 1 $\frac{2}{15}$, Grade 2 $\frac{2}{15}$, Grade 3 $\frac{2}{15}$, Grade 4 $\frac{2}{15}$, Grade 5 $\frac{3}{15}$, Grade 6 $\frac{3}{15}$. (1) What fraction of the school is Grade 6? (2) What fraction are Grades 1 and 2 together?

### Solution to Exercise 131

By **Definition 10.8.1 (Adding/Subtracting Fractions with Like Denominators)**: (1) Grade 6 is $\frac{3}{15}=\frac{1}{5}$; (2) Grades 1 and 2 together are $\frac{2}{15}+\frac{2}{15}=\frac{4}{15}$. $\blacksquare$

## Exercise 132

(Lesson: Unlike-Denominator Addition/Subtraction, Do It) Compute: $\frac{5}{8}+\frac{1}{3}$, $\frac{1}{4}+\frac{3}{8}$, $\frac{7}{10}-\frac{1}{5}$, $\frac{2}{3}-\frac{1}{2}$.

### Solution to Exercise 132

By **Definition 10.8.2 (Adding/Subtracting Fractions with Unlike Denominators)**: $\frac{5}{8}+\frac{1}{3}=\frac{15}{24}+\frac{8}{24}=\frac{23}{24}$; $\frac{1}{4}+\frac{3}{8}=\frac{2}{8}+\frac{3}{8}=\frac{5}{8}$; $\frac{7}{10}-\frac{1}{5}=\frac{7}{10}-\frac{2}{10}=\frac{5}{10}=\frac{1}{2}$; $\frac{2}{3}-\frac{1}{2}=\frac{4}{6}-\frac{3}{6}=\frac{1}{6}$. $\blacksquare$

## Exercise 133

(Lesson: Unlike-Denominator Addition/Subtraction, Exercise 24, Problem 3) Spring has come; a farmer waters fruit trees. On the first morning he waters $\frac{1}{4}$ of all trees, in the afternoon $\frac{3}{8}$, and on the second morning $\frac{3}{10}$. How much is watered in total? How much remains?

### Solution to Exercise 133

By **Definition 10.8.2 (Adding/Subtracting Fractions with Unlike Denominators)**, total watered is $\frac{1}{4}+\frac{3}{8}+\frac{3}{10}=\frac{10}{40}+\frac{15}{40}+\frac{12}{40}=\frac{37}{40}$; remaining is $1-\frac{37}{40}=\frac{3}{40}$. $\blacksquare$

## Exercise 134

(Lesson: Mixed Addition/Subtraction, Do It) Compute: $\frac{4}{5}-\frac{3}{10}+\frac{2}{3}$, $\frac{1}{2}+\left(\frac{1}{6}-\frac{1}{12}\right)$.

### Solution to Exercise 134

By **Definition 10.8.3 (Mixed Addition and Subtraction of Fractions)**: $\frac{4}{5}-\frac{3}{10}+\frac{2}{3}=\frac{24}{30}-\frac{9}{30}+\frac{20}{30}=\frac{35}{30}=\frac{7}{6}$; $\frac{1}{2}+\left(\frac{1}{6}-\frac{1}{12}\right)=\frac{6}{12}+\left(\frac{2}{12}-\frac{1}{12}\right)=\frac{6}{12}+\frac{1}{12}=\frac{7}{12}$. $\blacksquare$

## Exercise 135

(Lesson: Simplified Fraction Addition) Compute by a shortcut: $\frac{1}{3}+\frac{1}{5}+\frac{2}{3}$, $\frac{11}{12}-\frac{1}{6}-\frac{5}{6}$.

### Solution to Exercise 135

By **Definition 10.8.3 (Mixed Addition and Subtraction of Fractions)**, use commutative and associative laws: $\frac{1}{3}+\frac{1}{5}+\frac{2}{3}=\left(\frac{1}{3}+\frac{2}{3}\right)+\frac{1}{5}=1+\frac{1}{5}=1\frac{1}{5}$; $\frac{11}{12}-\frac{1}{6}-\frac{5}{6}=\frac{11}{12}-\left(\frac{1}{6}+\frac{5}{6}\right)=\frac{11}{12}-1=-\frac{1}{12}$ (per the textbook data). $\blacksquare$
