> **Definition 10.8.1** (Adding/Subtracting Fractions with Like Denominators) <br>To add or subtract fractions with like denominators, keep the denominator and only add or subtract the numerators. Reduce the result to lowest terms.
>
> **Definition 10.8.2** (Adding/Subtracting Fractions with Unlike Denominators) <br>To add or subtract fractions with unlike denominators, first find a common denominator, convert them to like denominators, then add or subtract as with like denominators.
>
> **Definition 10.8.3** (Mixed Addition and Subtraction of Fractions) <br>Mixed fraction addition/subtraction follows the same order as integers: left to right when there are no parentheses, and inside parentheses first when there are. The commutative and associative laws of integer addition also hold for fraction addition.

### Exercise 129

(Lesson: Like-Denominator Addition/Subtraction, Do It) Compute: $\dfrac{2}{9}+\dfrac{5}{9}$, $\dfrac{6}{7}-\dfrac{2}{7}$, $\dfrac{7}{10}-\dfrac{1}{10}$, $\dfrac{5}{8}+\dfrac{1}{8}$.

### Solution to Exercise 129

By **Definition 10.8.1** (Adding/Subtracting Fractions with Like Denominators): $\dfrac{2}{9}+\dfrac{5}{9}=\dfrac{7}{9}$; $\dfrac{6}{7}-\dfrac{2}{7}=\dfrac{4}{7}$; $\dfrac{7}{10}-\dfrac{1}{10}=\dfrac{6}{10}=\dfrac{3}{5}$; $\dfrac{5}{8}+\dfrac{1}{8}=\dfrac{6}{8}=\dfrac{3}{4}$. $\blacksquare$

### Exercise 130

(Lesson: Like-Denominator Addition/Subtraction, Exercise 23, Problem 1) Compute: $\dfrac{5}{12}+\dfrac{1}{12}$, $\dfrac{7}{8}-\dfrac{3}{8}$, $\dfrac{3}{10}+\dfrac{9}{10}$, $\dfrac{11}{15}-\dfrac{11}{15}$.

### Solution to Exercise 130

By **Definition 10.8.1** (Adding/Subtracting Fractions with Like Denominators): $\dfrac{5}{12}+\dfrac{1}{12}=\dfrac{6}{12}=\dfrac{1}{2}$; $\dfrac{7}{8}-\dfrac{3}{8}=\dfrac{4}{8}=\dfrac{1}{2}$; $\dfrac{3}{10}+\dfrac{9}{10}=\dfrac{12}{10}=\dfrac{6}{5}$; $\dfrac{11}{15}-\dfrac{11}{15}=0$. $\blacksquare$

### Exercise 131

(Lesson: Like-Denominator Addition/Subtraction, Exercise 23, Problem 5) The share of students in each grade of a primary school is: Grade 1 $\dfrac{2}{15}$, Grade 2 $\dfrac{2}{15}$, Grade 3 $\dfrac{2}{15}$, Grade 4 $\dfrac{2}{15}$, Grade 5 $\dfrac{3}{15}$, Grade 6 $\dfrac{3}{15}$. (1) What fraction of the school is Grade 6? (2) What fraction are Grades 1 and 2 together?

### Solution to Exercise 131

By **Definition 10.8.1** (Adding/Subtracting Fractions with Like Denominators): (1) Grade 6 is $\dfrac{3}{15}=\dfrac{1}{5}$; (2) Grades 1 and 2 together are $\dfrac{2}{15}+\dfrac{2}{15}=\dfrac{4}{15}$. $\blacksquare$

### Exercise 132

(Lesson: Unlike-Denominator Addition/Subtraction, Do It) Compute: $\dfrac{5}{8}+\dfrac{1}{3}$, $\dfrac{1}{4}+\dfrac{3}{8}$, $\dfrac{7}{10}-\dfrac{1}{5}$, $\dfrac{2}{3}-\dfrac{1}{2}$.

### Solution to Exercise 132

By **Definition 10.8.2** (Adding/Subtracting Fractions with Unlike Denominators): $\dfrac{5}{8}+\dfrac{1}{3}=\dfrac{15}{24}+\dfrac{8}{24}=\dfrac{23}{24}$; $\dfrac{1}{4}+\dfrac{3}{8}=\dfrac{2}{8}+\dfrac{3}{8}=\dfrac{5}{8}$; $\dfrac{7}{10}-\dfrac{1}{5}=\dfrac{7}{10}-\dfrac{2}{10}=\dfrac{5}{10}=\dfrac{1}{2}$; $\dfrac{2}{3}-\dfrac{1}{2}=\dfrac{4}{6}-\dfrac{3}{6}=\dfrac{1}{6}$. $\blacksquare$

### Exercise 133

(Lesson: Unlike-Denominator Addition/Subtraction, Exercise 24, Problem 3) Spring has come; a farmer waters fruit trees. On the first morning he waters $\dfrac{1}{4}$ of all trees, in the afternoon $\dfrac{3}{8}$, and on the second morning $\dfrac{3}{10}$. How much is watered in total? How much remains?

### Solution to Exercise 133

By **Definition 10.8.2** (Adding/Subtracting Fractions with Unlike Denominators), total watered is $\dfrac{1}{4}+\dfrac{3}{8}+\dfrac{3}{10}=\dfrac{10}{40}+\dfrac{15}{40}+\dfrac{12}{40}=\dfrac{37}{40}$; remaining is $1-\dfrac{37}{40}=\dfrac{3}{40}$. $\blacksquare$

### Exercise 134

(Lesson: Mixed Addition/Subtraction, Do It) Compute: $\dfrac{4}{5}-\dfrac{3}{10}+\dfrac{2}{3}$, $\dfrac{1}{2}+\left(\dfrac{1}{6}-\dfrac{1}{12}\right)$.

### Solution to Exercise 134

By **Definition 10.8.3** (Mixed Addition and Subtraction of Fractions): $\dfrac{4}{5}-\dfrac{3}{10}+\dfrac{2}{3}=\dfrac{24}{30}-\dfrac{9}{30}+\dfrac{20}{30}=\dfrac{35}{30}=\dfrac{7}{6}$; $\dfrac{1}{2}+\left(\dfrac{1}{6}-\dfrac{1}{12}\right)=\dfrac{6}{12}+\left(\dfrac{2}{12}-\dfrac{1}{12}\right)=\dfrac{6}{12}+\dfrac{1}{12}=\dfrac{7}{12}$. $\blacksquare$

### Exercise 135

(Lesson: Simplified Fraction Addition) Compute by a shortcut: $\dfrac{1}{3}+\dfrac{1}{5}+\dfrac{2}{3}$, $\dfrac{11}{12}-\dfrac{1}{6}-\dfrac{5}{6}$.

### Solution to Exercise 135

By **Definition 10.8.3** (Mixed Addition and Subtraction of Fractions), use commutative and associative laws: $\dfrac{1}{3}+\dfrac{1}{5}+\dfrac{2}{3}=\left(\dfrac{1}{3}+\dfrac{2}{3}\right)+\dfrac{1}{5}=1+\dfrac{1}{5}=1\dfrac{1}{5}$; $\dfrac{11}{12}-\dfrac{1}{6}-\dfrac{5}{6}=\dfrac{11}{12}-\left(\dfrac{1}{6}+\dfrac{5}{6}\right)=\dfrac{11}{12}-1=-\dfrac{1}{12}$ (per the textbook data). $\blacksquare$
