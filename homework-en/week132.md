> **Definition 11.5.1 (圆各部分的名称)**
> 
> 圆规针尖固定的一点叫圆心, 用 $O$ 表示; 连接圆心和圆上任意一点的线段叫半径, 用 $r$ 表示; 通过圆心并且两端都在圆上的线段叫直径, 用 $d$ 表示. 同一个圆里 $d=2r$. 墨子云: "圆, 一中同长也".
> **Definition 11.5.2 (弧, 扇形, 圆心角)**
> 
> 圆上两点之间的部分叫弧; 一条弧和经过这条弧两端的两条半径所围成的图形叫扇形; 顶点在圆心的角叫圆心角. 同一个圆中, 扇形的大小与圆心角的大小有关.
> **Definition 11.5.3 (圆周率)**
> 
> 任意一个圆的周长除以它的直径, 所得的商是一个固定的数, 叫圆周率, 用 $\pi$ 表示. 它是无限不循环小数, 计算时通常取 $\pi\approx3.14$.
> **Definition 11.5.4 (圆的周长)**
> 
> 围成圆的曲线的长叫圆的周长, $C=\pi d=2\pi r$.
> **Definition 11.5.5 (圆的面积)**
> 
> 圆所占平面的大小叫圆的面积. 把圆等分拼成近似长方形, 长为 $\pi r$, 宽为 $r$, 故 $S=\pi r^2$.

## Exercise 1

(Lesson: Area of a Circle, Exercise 12 Review Problem 2) Find circumference and area: $d=10\,\text{cm}$; $r=3\,\text{cm}$.

### Solution to Exercise 1

### Solution to Exercise 2

### Solution to Exercise 3

## Exercise 5

(Lesson: Area of a Circle, Exercise 12 Review Problem 6) In both figures the large radius equals the small diameter; find shaded area. What do you find?

### Solution to Exercise 5

Let small radius $r$, large $2r$. Shaded $=\pi\left(2r\right)^2-\pi r^2=3\pi r^2$, i.e. $\frac{3}{4}$ of the large circle. $\blacksquare$

## Exercise 8

(Lesson: Area of a Circle, Exercise 12 Integrated Problem 9) A sports field has semicircular ends and a rectangular middle, length $100\,\text{m}$, width $32\,\text{m}$. Perimeter? Area?

### Solution to Exercise 8

Perimeter $=$ circle $+$ two straights $=3.14\times32+100\times2=100.48+200=300.48\,\text{m}$. Area $=3.14\times16^2+100\times32=803.84+3200=4003.84\,\text{m}^2$. $\blacksquare$

## Exercise 9

(Lesson: Area of a Circle, Exercise 12 Integrated Problem 10) A petal-shaped doorway is bounded by $4$ semicircles each of diameter $1\,\text{m}$. Its perimeter and area?

### Solution to Exercise 9

Perimeter $=4\times\frac{1}{2}\times3.14\times1=6.28\,\text{m}$. Area $=$ square $1\times1+2$ full circles $=1+3.14\times0.5^2\times2=1+1.57=2.57\,\text{m}^2$. $\blacksquare$

## Exercise 10

(Lesson: Area of a Circle, Exercise 12 Integrated Problem 11) Two ring-shaped Tulou: outer $34\,\text{m}$ inner $14\,\text{m}$; outer $26\,\text{m}$ inner $14\,\text{m}$. Difference in floor area?

$3.14\times\left(17^2-7^2\right)-3.14\times\left(13^2-7^2\right)=3.14\times\left(289-49-169+49\right)=3.14\times120=376.8\,\text{m}^2$. $\blacksquare$

## Exercise 11

(Lesson: Area of a Circle, Exercise 12 Integrated Problem 12) A circle's perimeter is $62.8\,\text{m}$; after radius increases by $2\,\text{m}$, how much does area grow?

### Solution to Exercise 11

Old $r=62.8\div3.14\div2=10\,\text{m}$, new $r=12\,\text{m}$. Increase $3.14\times\left(12^2-10^2\right)=3.14\times44=138.16\,\text{m}^2$. $\blacksquare$

## Exercise 12

(Lesson: Area of a Circle, Exercise 12 Integrated Problem 13) The park has two equal semicircular lawns, each perimeter $128.5\,\text{m}$; total area?

### Solution to Exercise 12

Semicircle perimeter $=\pi r+2r=r\left(\pi+2\right)=128.5$, $r=128.5\div5.14=25\,\text{m}$. Two semicircles make one circle: area $3.14\times25^2=1962.5\,\text{m}^2$. $\blacksquare$

## Exercise 13

(Lesson: Area of a Circle, Exercise 12 Extension Problem 14) A piece cut from an annulus is an annular sector. Find its area: $r=4\,\text{dm}$, $R=5\,\text{dm}$ (central angle $90^{\circ}$).

### Solution to Exercise 13

Annular sector area $=\frac{90}{360}\times3.14\times\left(5^2-4^2\right)=\frac{1}{4}\times3.14\times9=7.065\,\text{dm}^2$. $\blacksquare$

## Exercise 15

(Lesson: Area of a Circle, Exercise 12 Extension Problem 16) A rectangular building with a post at the corner; the dog is tied with a $4\,\text{m}$ rope. How large is its roaming area?

### Solution to Exercise 15

The dog sweeps radius $4\,\text{m}$ around the corner; roaming area is $\frac{3}{4}$ of a circle: $\frac{3}{4}\times3.14\times4^2=37.68\,\text{m}^2$. $\blacksquare$

## Exercise 16

(Lesson: Area of a Circle, Exercise 12 Extension Problem 17) (1) A rope is $31.4\,\text{m}$; enclose a plot on the playground. How to enclose the largest area? (2) Why is a yurt's base circular? Why are most plant root/stem cross-sections circular?

### Solution to Exercise 16

(1) When $31.4$ is the circle's perimeter, $r=5\,\text{m}$, area $78.5\,\text{m}^2$; for a fixed perimeter the circle maximizes area. (2) With the same material, a circular base gives the most area, hence yurts are round; round root/stem sections aid water transport and save material. $\blacksquare$
