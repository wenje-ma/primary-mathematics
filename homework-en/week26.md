> **Definition 3.5.1 (厘米)**
> 
> "厘米"是一个统一的长度单位, 可以用"$\mathrm{cm}$"表示. 量比较短的物体, 可以用厘米作单位. 食指宽,某种便利贴宽大约是 $1$ 厘米.

> **Definition 3.5.2 (米)**
> 
> 量比较长的物体, 通常用"米"作单位, 可以用"$\mathrm{m}$"表示. $1$ 米 $=100$ 厘米.

> **Definition 3.5.3 (线段)**
> 
> 拉直的一根线可以看作一条线段. 线段是直的, 可以量出长度. 黑板的边,桌子的边,书的边都可以看作线段.

> **Definition 3.5.4 (测量方法)**
> 
> 量物体长度时, 把尺子的刻度 $0$ 对准物体的一端, 再看物体的另一端对着刻度几, 就是几厘米.

> **Definition 3.6.1 (身体尺)**
> 
> 拃,步,庹,脚,指,手,肘等是古代使用过的长度单位, 又称为"身体尺". 没有尺子或不方便用尺子时, 可以选用合适的身体尺测量长度.

> **Definition 3.6.2 (1 拃)**
> 
> 把手指用力张开, 大拇指指尖到中指指尖的距离是 $1$ 拃.

> **Definition 3.6.3 (1 庹)**
> 
> 两臂左右平伸, 两个中指指尖之间的距离是 $1$ 庹; $1$ 庹的长和身高差不多.

## Exercise 6

(Lesson: Solving Problems, Example, Flagpole) Is the height of the school flagpole $13$ centimeters or $13$ meters? How do you tell?

### Solution to Exercise 6

By **Definition 3.5.1 (Centimeter)** and **Definition 3.5.2 (Meter)**, $13$ centimeters is about $1$ hand-span, and a flagpole cannot be that short; even a new pencil is more than $13$ centimeters, while a child is a bit over $1$ meter tall, and $10$ children's heights stacked up are about as tall as the flagpole, so the flagpole is $13$ meters high.

$\blacksquare$

## Exercise 19

(Lesson: Practice, Problem 8) A wire: first half of it is used, then half of what remains is used, and finally $3$ meters are left. How long was the wire originally?

### Solution to Exercise 19

By **Definition 3.5.2 (Meter)**, work backward: in the end $3$ meters are left, which is half of what remained before the second use, so before the second use there were $3\times2=6$ (meters); these $6$ meters are half of the original, so originally the wire was $6\times2=12$ (meters) long.

$\blacksquare$
